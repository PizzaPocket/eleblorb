# Crossroads Fishing Village: Architecture Briefs

Status: building briefs for review (2026-10-06). Read with `fishing_village.md`
(community and charter) and `fishing_village_layout.md` (approved layout).
Footprints, positions and owners come from the layout; this document fixes each
building's form. Interiors and planting follow.

## 0. Revision (2026-10-06): a village that floats as well as stands

The first draft of this brief had one houseboat among fifteen piled structures,
so the village read as a pier town. A lake community of boat people should live
on the water as much as over it. The census, owners and trade stay as approved;
what changes is how three households are housed.

| Household | Was | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vale (Jori, Osei, Tavi) | piled house on the Heron shelf | **family houseboat** moored off the catch deck's west side, with its own working deck | seasonal fishers from the far shore; their home is a boat, which is why the smokehouse deck stays separate and downwind |
| Sen (Asha, Rian) | one house with Rian's side deck | piled **clinic and school house** as before, plus Rian's **shell-works barge** moored in the water court south of the spine at `x -20..-12, z -3..0.5`, reached by a short gangway from the spine | the saw and sealing noise leaves the clinic; the barge rides on lines and a gangway |
| Mor (Leena, Ivo, Sela) | one guest houseboat | the **guest houseboat** plus **Ivo's ferry-launch**, a roofed cargo launch with a cabin | the ferry is the village's way out; it becomes a real vessel with a berth and a cabin |
| Aran (Mai, Salim, Dala, Pree) | piled house and pontoon yard | unchanged, with Pree's **herb floats** as small moored rafts | the oldest house, on the first piles |
| Venn (Nara, Mateo, Lio) | piled house with shop veranda | unchanged (Lio keeps his loft under the hip) | the shop veranda must meet the landing |

That makes **four floating structures** (the Mor and Vale houseboats, Rian's
barge, Ivo's launch with a cabin) beside the Aran pontoon, the herb floats and
the boats, against five piled houses and work decks (Venn, Sen, Aran, the
cistern house and the net shed) and the pavilion that tie into the islets. Piled buildings
are the older, fixed heart; floating ones hold the families who came later and
move seasonally. Floating structures are moored to pile clusters and rock rings
by lines and flexible gangways, never to the open lake bed.

`FishingVillagePlan` now carries these moorings (2026-10-06): the Vale
houseboat floats at `x 26..40, z -10..-3` north of Heron Rock, joined to the
Heron landing by a gangway; Rian's barge sits in the water court on a spine
gangway; Ivo's launch keeps the ferry berth, with a cabin. Every one still needs a style charter entry in
`docs/style_charters.md` (it has none for this village yet) before it is built.

## 1. Kit of parts

The primary influence is the working timber jetty settlement (George Town's
Clan Jetties), translated: light timber frames on piles, broad roofs, deep
shade and open walls. The house colours are the current draft's terracotta,
teal, ochre, violet, coral red and green, applied by household.

### Invariants

| Element | Term | Specification |
|---|---|---|
| Structural module | bay | 3.0 m between posts (light timber; a little under the town's 3.2 m cell so the approved footprints hold) |
| Foundation | piles | squarish SuperEgg timber piles 0.3 m, braced in pairs below the deck; pile caps visible at deck edge; on the rock shelves only |
| Floor datum | — | public decks W + 0.50 m; house floors W + 0.75 m, a 0.25 m ramped threshold up from the deck |
| Frame | post and beam | exposed squarish posts on every bay line, a ring beam at 2.9 m |
| Walls | infill panels | light timber boards, woven rattan screen panels (basket weave) and hinged shutters between posts; never a sealed box |
| Roofs, main | hip or gable | one pitch band, 30° to 34°; deep eaves of 0.9 m; ridge vents |
| Roofs, verandas | pent | 15° to 18°, falling away from the wall |
| Roof finish | — | timber shingle in sun-faded tones; ridge caps in the household colour |
| Openings | superellipse | doors 1.2 × 2.4 m with half-height lower leaves for air; window openings with top-hung shutters that prop open as sunshades |
| Rainwater | gutters and jars | every roof drains by a gutter into glazed jars or a household tank; drinking water never from the lake |
| Sanitation | dry closet | a sealed dry closet on each house's service side, emptied ashore |
| Rails | balustrade | a top rail on slender posts wherever a deck edge is more than 0.6 m above water beside a route |
| Signature motif | rope-bound post | rope wrapped around the threshold posts of every house and work deck |
| Night light | — | lamps hung under eaves and on junction posts; no lights on sticks |

### Household palettes

Applied to wall panels, shutters, ridge caps and the household's boat, with
natural timber for the frame.

| Household | Colour | Accent |
|---|---|---|
| Venn | teal | ochre shutters |
| Aran | ochre | green |
| Vale | coral red | natural timber |
| Sen | violet | cream |
| Mor | terracotta | teal |
| Shared buildings | natural timber | green ridge caps |

### Exclusions

Sealed boxes; Peranakan shophouse facades; clan temples or sacred roofs; brick
or masonry walls; lights on poles; uniform brown.

Footprints in the approved layout include eaves. Where a building below needs
slightly more, its footprint may grow by up to 1 m on a side, always keeping at
least 3 m to its neighbours.

## 2. Building briefs

Each brief: masses, roof, base, openings, rooms, colour, history, variation.

### Arrival landing and market deck (public)

- 14 × 14 m pile deck at `x -42..-28`, `z -12..+2`.
- A **market shelter** of four posts with a pent roof along the landing's north
  edge, where notices and the community board hang (pictures only).
- Ferry berth on the west edge; Nara's skiff berth at the north-west corner;
  swim exit 1 on the south edge.
- **Variation:** the only large open deck; a bench along the rail facing the
  shore.

### Venn house and lake shop (Nara, Mateo, Lio)

- **Masses:** a main house 6 × 4.5 m (two bays by one and a half) under a hipped
  roof, and a front veranda 9 × 2.4 m under a pent roof facing the landing.
- **Shop:** the veranda is the shop: the counter along its front edge with the
  ware display; Nara stands behind it.
- **Rooms:** living and kitchen room; a sleeping room for Nara and Mateo; Lio's
  loft under the hip, reached by a ramp; a dive-gear store on the service side.
- **Colour:** teal walls, ochre shutters.
- **History:** Nara's family's house; the shop veranda was added when she took
  over the trade.
- **Variation:** the only house with a shop front; Lio's lamp in the loft window.

### Boatwright slip (Mateo)

- A sloped haul-out ramp running west into the water, 4 m wide, 12 m long, with
  rollers; an open **tool shelter** 6 × 4 m beside it under a gable roof;
  timber racks along its back wall; one boat under repair on the slip.
- **Variation:** the only gable roof on the west side; sawdust on the deck.

### Sen house, clinic and school room (Asha, Rian)

- **Masses:** a three-bay main range 9 × 6 m under a hipped roof, with a covered
  porch on the spine side.
- **Rooms:** the clinic room by the front door (cot, medicine shelves, a washing
  table); the school room behind it (a low table, three stools, a slate wall);
  Asha's and Rian's sleeping rooms at the rear; Asha's record shelves.
- **Rian's deck:** a side deck 3 × 7 m to the east with a lean-to roof, his
  cutting and sealing bench, rope coils and shell bins; reached from inside.
- **Colour:** violet walls, cream shutters.
- **History:** built new when Asha and Rian stayed after the storm season; the
  side deck added for Rian's trade.
- **Variation:** the longest house; the clinic door with a carved knot mark.

### Cistern house and shared stores (village; Leena keeps the stores)

- A 6 × 6 m masonry-footed timber house against Anvil Rock's foot, where the
  seep is gathered into a covered cistern; a public tap and bucket ledge on the
  spine side; the stores room behind with shelves of rope, pitch, lamp oil and
  dried food.
- **Roof:** hipped, gutters from every face into the cistern.
- **Colour:** natural timber, green ridge.
- **Variation:** the only building tied into the rock; always wet at the base.

### Net shed and gear loft (Salim, shared with Jori)

- A 6 × 6 m open-sided shed under a high gable for hanging nets to dry, with a
  floored loft along one side for traps and floats.
- **Colour:** natural timber, green ridge.
- **Variation:** nets hanging inside like curtains; the tallest roof on the
  spine.

### Communal pavilion (Leena)

- **Masses:** an open-sided pavilion 12 × 6 m, four bays by two, under a large
  hipped roof with a raised ridge vent for the heat of shared meals.
- **Use:** long tables and benches for meals; a repair bench along one end; the
  storm muster point; a bell on a post at the corner.
- **Swim exit 2** leaves its south edge into the water court.
- **Colour:** natural timber with green ridge and fascia.
- **Variation:** the only large roof without walls; the village's centre.

### Aran house and pearl yard (Mai, Salim, Dala, Pree)

- **Masses:** a two-bay house 6 × 6 m under a hipped roof, with a deep veranda
  2.5 m wide facing the water court where Dala reads the weather.
- **Wet stair:** a broad stair-ramp from the south side down to the pearl yard
  pontoon.
- **Rooms:** living room; kitchen on the service side; Mai and Salim's room;
  Dala's room nearest the veranda; Pree's corner with her herb floats tied
  outside.
- **Pearl yard:** a 10 × 5 m pontoon with clean grading tables under a light
  shade canopy, basket racks and a fresh-water rinse jar.
- **Colour:** ochre walls, green shutters.
- **History:** the oldest house in the village, on Dala's generation's first
  piles; repaired many times, so its boards are of several ages.
- **Variation:** mixed-age boards; the herb floats; the weather veranda.

### Vale house (Jori, Osei, Tavi)

- **Masses:** a three-bay house 9 × 6 m under a gable roof with its ridge east to
  west, so smoke from the catch deck slides past.
- **Rooms:** living and kitchen room; Jori and Osei's room; Tavi's room with his
  cork floats; a gear store.
- **Colour:** coral red walls, natural shutters.
- **Variation:** the only gable house; floats drying on the railings.

### Catch deck, drying shelter and smokehouse (Osei)

- A 8 × 12 m working deck on Heron's east shelf: a sorting table and rinse
  trough at the berth edge; a **drying shelter** with screened slatted sides
  and a pent roof; a small **smokehouse** 3 × 3 m with a stone hearth and a
  short flue, at the downwind end.
- **Colour:** natural timber, smoke-darkened.
- **Variation:** the only fire in the village; the working boat's berth.

### Mor guest houseboat (Leena, Ivo, Sela)

As specified in `fishing_village.md`: an 18 × 7 m shallow-draft timber vessel
with a long weather roof and deep side eaves; covered arrival deck, common cabin
and galley, central party dormitory, two guest cabins, the family's stern cabin
and a contained dry toilet. In terracotta with teal trim. Its roof is the same
30° to 34° pitch as the houses so it reads as part of the village.

### Ocean portal landing (public)

- A 9 × 8 m pile deck against Heron Rock's south face, its gate standing on a
  low step facing the portal spur, 3 m of clear deck behind it, and swim exit 3
  on its south edge.
- **Variation:** the only deck without a roof; lamps on its two corner posts.

### Swim exits (three)

Each 3.4 m wide, timber-planked with cross battens for grip, rails on both
sides above the water, its foot at the derived height in `fishing_village_layout.md`.
