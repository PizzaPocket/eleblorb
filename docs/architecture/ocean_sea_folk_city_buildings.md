# The Sea Folk City: Architecture Briefs

Status: building briefs for review (2026-10-06). Read with
`ocean_sea_folk_city.md` (community, culture, charter, dress) and
`ocean_sea_folk_city_layout.md` (layout). Positions and owners come from the
layout.

## 1. Kit of parts

**Atlantean modern** (about 70 percent): continuous shells, curved glass and
orichalcum structure. **Minoan** (about 25 percent), limited to named elements:
downward-tapering columns, light wells, spiral and wave bands, and fresco bands
of dolphins and octopus. **Accent:** living pearl lamps and braided kelp. No
building uses Greek temple forms; those belong to the Sky Kingdom.

### Invariants

| Element | Specification |
|---|---|
| Shell | continuous superellipse shells finished in **nacre**, iridescent white-teal; walls and roof are one curved form, never a box with a lid |
| Structure | **orichalcum** ribs (red-gold) on 3.2 m centres, visible inside and out where the shell is glazed |
| Glass | large curved panes between ribs, clear or faintly green |
| Base | a nacre-faced plinth socketed into the levelled floor, 0.6 m high for wet buildings, 3 m for air halls (so tunnels and swimmers pass) |
| Columns | Minoan form: wider at the top than the base, with a round cushion capital; painted deep red or ocean blue; squarish SuperEgg shafts as the engine's language requires |
| Bands | a spiral or wave band at the shell's springing line on every building, the signature motif |
| Frescoes | dolphins, octopus, fish and sea flowers, inside air halls and the castle only |
| Light | living pearl lamps at every door and junction; light pipes bring daylight down to the civic ring |
| Palette | nacre white and teal 60 percent; prism green, ocean blue and violet light 30 percent; orichalcum 10 percent |

### Wet and dry buildings

- **Wet buildings** (the castle, homes, the Lamp House, the Reef House, the
  lookout) are full of water. Sea folk swim in and rest floating. Openings are
  at least 3 m clear and rooms at least 4 m tall so swimmers move in three
  dimensions. There are no hung doors: woven-kelp screens give privacy and are
  swum through.
- **Dry buildings** (the three air halls and the tunnels) hold compressed air:
  a moon pool 4 m across in the floor, a ramp out of it, nacre floors, frescoes,
  and furniture for people on legs. Each registers as a dry and breathable
  volume.

### Exclusions

Greek temple orders and pediments; horns of consecration, double axes and
other sacred emblems; ruins and broken columns; anything resembling a sunken
human city; boxes.

## 2. Building briefs

### Tidehall, the castle (Asaru, Ikara, Tudaro)

- **Keep:** the luminous teal and prism-green palette, the four corner towers
  with their prism crowns, the open, traversable audience hall and its status
  as the brightest landmark (non-regression).
- **Change:** rebuilt as continuous nacre-and-teal shells on a 2 m mound, its
  entrance turned to `+z`, approached up a broad ramp between two Minoan
  columns.
- **Audience hall:** a tall wet hall with a central light well rising to a glass
  oculus fed by a light pipe; Asaru's seat of nacre at the far end; dolphin
  frescoes along the walls; room enough for the Tidekeeper's challenge fight.
- **Rooms:** Asaru's chambers behind the hall; Ikara's and Tudaro's rooms in two
  of the towers; a guard post at the entrance.
- **Variation:** the only building with towers and crowns; the only fresco room
  that is wet.

### The Exchange (air hall; Maresa, with Rodasu and Dakuro)

- A dome 18 m across, 9 m high, on a 3 m plinth, glazed between orichalcum ribs.
- **Inside:** the moon pool and its ramp; Maresa's counter (Nautilus Shells);
  Rodasu's counter (Watering Cans); a trading floor with tables; Dakuro's chart
  room behind a screen; a fresco band of trading ships and fish.
- **Tunnel junctions:** the civic tunnel and the avenue tunnel leave from its
  sides.
- **Variation:** the largest dome; the busiest.

### The guest hall (air hall; Isaro; rest point)

- A dome 16 m across, 8 m high, on a 3 m plinth.
- **Inside:** the moon pool; a common room with low couches; four dry bedrooms
  around the dome's edge, one large enough for the party, where the hero wakes;
  Isaro's counter by the pool; a **washroom** between two of the bedrooms,
  reached from the common room, for guests on legs.
- **Washroom (modern):** a vacuum-flush toilet of the kind ships and aircraft
  use, sparing with water, in a nacre shell with an orichalcum flush plate; a
  glass washbasin with a tap and a mirror; a shower. Waste goes to a sealed
  holding tank in the plinth, which is pumped out to a treatment vault at the
  city's edge, never into the sea.
- **Variation:** the warmest light; the only dome with rooms partitioned off.
- **Audit (2026-10-07):**
  - **Headroom.** A dome 8 m high is low at its rim. Set the four bedrooms
    and the washroom inside the radius where the shell still clears 2.4 m over
    the floor, and use the strip between them and the shell for storage under
    the slope, never as a route.
  - **Fit.** The rooms ring the dome's interior of about 200 m² with the 4 m
    moon pool, the ramp out of it and the common room at the centre. That
    fits.
  - **Access.** Each bedroom and the washroom opens off the common room, the
    house's shared hall, which is legitimate.

### The Glassworks (air hall; Kunei and Damaku)

- A dome 16 m across on a socketed plinth at the vent's edge, its lower glazing
  smoked by heat.
- **Inside:** the moon pool; a glass furnace heated by the vent through an
  orichalcum heat exchanger; Kunei's blowing floor; Damaku's tunnel-segment
  workshop, with a finished curved pane waiting on a rack.
- **Variation:** the only dome with work clutter; a visible warm glow at the
  head of the main avenue.

### Glass tunnels

- A superellipse glass tube 4.5 m across on orichalcum ribs every 3.2 m, its
  floor 3 m wide and flat, 3.2 m headroom, on slender supports holding it 3 m
  above the floor.
- A junction moon pool at 70 m on the avenue tunnel.
- **Variation:** none; they are infrastructure, and read as one system.

### Lamp House (Lunira, Korasi, Mira)

- A wet building 12 × 10 m with a long glazed front facing the civic ring.
- **Inside:** open trays where young pearl lamps are grown; Korasi's reading
  bench where he watches them for storm signs; the family's resting alcoves.
- **Variation:** glows from inside all night.

### School court (Munaja)

- An open court 16 × 12 m ringed by Minoan columns under a shallow shell canopy.
- **Use:** where the young practise balance and swimming before the open sea;
  a few floating rings and markers.

### Light pipes (Kaluja)

- Four shafts 1.6 m across of orichalcum and glass rising from the civic ring to
  floating glass collectors at the surface; the lower 6 m of each glows with
  piped daylight.

### Reef House workshop (Talisa, Arimu, Kaluja)

- A wet building 12 × 9 m in the garden ring.
- **Inside:** specimen racks, Arimu's night-reef records on nacre tablets,
  Talisa's sorting benches.

### Kraken lookout (Ikara)

- A slender tower 14 m tall at the city's edge, with a glazed watch chamber at
  the top facing the Kraken's route.

### Homes (sixteen)

Each household's homes share a type; individual homes vary within it.

| Type | Size | Form | Used by |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Single chamber** | 8 × 7 m, 5 m tall | one nacre shell with a glazed front and a kelp-screened door | Exchange and Reef House households |
| **Double chamber** | 11 × 7 m | two shells joined by a short passage: living chamber and resting chamber | Garden House and Current House households |
| **Workshop home** | 10 × 8 m | a resting chamber with a work chamber facing the avenue | Glassworks household; the Lamp House family's home beside the Lamp House |

- Every home: resting alcoves in nacre niches, woven-kelp screens, a pearl lamp
  at the door, braided kelp on the shell, and the household's work visible at
  the entrance.
- **Variation axes:** type, the colour of its pearl lamp, the kelp on its shell,
  the tail colours of its residents echoed in a band at the door, and its trade
  at the entrance (sea flowers, shells, charts, scale cloth).

## Fixed, proposed and open

**Fixed:** the layout; the castle's palette, towers, crowns and open audience
hall; the existing inn's role and price.

**Proposed:** the Atlantean modern kit with Minoan elements; wet and dry
building rules; every building's form; three home types.

**Open:** none blocking.
