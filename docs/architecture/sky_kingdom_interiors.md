# Sky Kingdom: Interiors and Cloud Planting

Status: interior and planting briefs for review (2026-10-06). Read with
`sky_kingdom.md` (people, materials), `sky_kingdom_layout.md` (approved
layout) and `sky_kingdom_buildings.md` (kit and building forms).

## 1. Furnishing a leisured cloud people

Tempestars are not human, so their rooms are not human rooms with gold on top:

- **Rest:** they float, so they rest on **couches of set cloud** held in gold
  frames, reclining as at a Roman banquet. No beds with legs.
- **Nourishment:** gold dew bowls, nectar jars, ambrosia on gold plates carried
  by tripods. No hearths, stoves or chimneys: nothing is cooked over fire.
- **Washing:** shallow basins of fresh dew in gold, set at a comfortable height
  for a floating body.
- **Storage:** quartz niches in the screen walls, gold-bound chests of set cloud,
  robe presses.
- **Light:** gold vessels and quartz that glow after dusk; daylight through
  arches and oculi. No candles, lamps on posts or open flame (except the
  lightning in Chrysa's forge).
- **Textiles:** woven cloud as curtains, cushions and wall hangings, in each
  court's pastel.
- **Furniture height:** tables and couches float about 1 m above the floor,
  where a Tempestar's hands are; party members and the hero walk beneath and
  around them, so every route keeps 2 m clear under floating pieces or goes
  around them.
- **Signature:** gold binding rings on every couch, chest and basin.

Per the `interior-design` skill, each room carries at least three things that
belong only to its occupant.

## 3. Cloud planting

Plants grow from cloud and are made of the same cloud material, with colour
only in fruit and blossom (see `sky_kingdom.md`, section 5). Each planting has a
job and, where tended, a tender.

| Island | Planting | Tender | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dawn Gate cloud** | two tier pines framing the gate; puff-shrub hedges along the forecourt | — | frame the arrival |
| **Aethra** | tier pines along the axis from the propylon to the Hall of Mist; one old cloud peach beside the nectar terrace; puff-shrub hedges clipped square around the forecourt; sky lilies in a pool before the hall | Drosia keeps the peach | formality and precedence |
| **Koinon** | a ring of tier pines around the Hall of Assembly, one per seat; sky lilies on a pool in the philosopher's court; cloud willows at the observatory's foot | Archeia | solemnity; a ring that echoes the empty seats |
| **Pantao** | the **Peach Garden**: loose groves of cloud peaches in their own orchard, the first-peach tree on a low mound; cloud wisteria trained along the banquet hall's eave; undercloud vines with pale gold berries hanging below the island | Chunlu | food, the first peach, and display |
| **Chrysa** | almost bare: a few hardy puff shrubs sheltered behind the residence; nothing near the lightning | — | a working court has no time for gardens |
| **Lyria** | cloud willows around the odeon whose strands move with the wind harps; cloud wisteria over the poet's residence | Elegon, carelessly | music and shade |
| **Dromos** | puff-shrub hedges ringing the inner field; a single tier pine at the finish line | Stephane | a clear course and a mark to race to |

Rules:

- Plant in groves and drifts of one kind, never one of each.
- Keep routes, landing forecourts, the racing track, the Koinon void and the
  forge clear.
- Trunks and substantial branches collide; foliage, willow strands, blossom and
  vines are decorative.

## Fixed, proposed and open

**Fixed:** the approved layout; the kit and building forms; no fire except
Chrysa's lightning; cloud material for plants.

**Proposed:** non-human furnishing (floating couches, dew basins, gold
vessels, woven cloud); every room's furnishing and personal items; planting by
island with tenders.

**Open:** none blocking.
