# Crossroads Fishing Village: Interior Design Briefs

Status: for review (2026-10-06). Written after, and fixed by, the architectural
designs in `fishing_village_building_designs.md`; the people are in
`fishing_village.md`. Per the interior-design skill each room gets an occupant
brief, a function, its anchors and three things only its occupant would own. The
plan, doors, swings and services are fixed inputs. Nothing here moves a wall.

Rules for every room: furniture is placed through `RoomLayout` so nothing
stands in a door swing or a route; pieces are SuperEgg parts; wear and light are
layered; no text anywhere (symbols and colour only). Warm light is lamplight
hung under the beams. The light and heat in this village come from oil lamps,
and the only open fire is the smokehouse's. Cooking is on a clay brazier with a
smoke hood in each galley, vented through the ridge.

Shared kit: rope-bound threshold posts, rattan panel walls, timber boards to the
waist, mats woven in the household colour, hanging gourd and glazed rain jars,
a peg rail by every door, a hand-lamp on a hook, a basket for shoes.

## 1. Venn house and lake shop

**Occupants.** Nara (diver, dock-warden, shopkeeper), Mateo (boatwright), Lio
(nearly an adult, apprenticed to both).

- **Shop veranda.** Receive. A long counter along the south edge: the lake wares
  (helmet, compass, pitch torch, bread, berries) laid out on a dark cloth, a
  brass hand scale, a bell on a cord. Nara stands behind it; a stool for slow
  hours; a diving helmet on a peg behind her. *Only Nara's:* a row of knotted
  cords, one for each dive that went wrong; a salt-stiff bag of tokens she buys
  shells with; a child-sized helmet from the first dive she taught.
- **Living room and kitchen.** Eat and cook. A clay brazier with a hood against
  the east wall, a low table with floor cushions, shelves of glazed crockery, a
  chest of ropes. *Only the family's:* Mateo's chisels in a wrapped roll; a
  child's drawing of a ferry pinned above the brazier; a broken compass in a bowl.
- **Sleeping room.** Sleep. A built bed against the west wall under the window,
  a sea chest at its foot (hinge toward the bed), pegs for oilskins, a hammock
  folded over a rail for hot nights.
- **Lio's loft.** Sleep and study. A low bed under the hip's slope, a window
  with his lamp, a rack of practice knots, a shell collection graded by size.
  Reached by the ramp; clear landing at both ends.
- **Dive-gear store.** A drying rack of suits, three helmets on a shelf, a
  workbench with the housing in pieces (Mateo builds, Rian seals).

## 2. Boatwright slip and tool shelter

**Occupant.** Mateo. Work. Timber racks, a plane and adze on pegs, a steam box
made from a barrel, planks on trestles, a half-planked dinghy on the slip with
tar pots beside it, sawdust underfoot. *Only Mateo's:* a carved sternpost from
the damaged cargo boat he arrived on, set over the bench like a trophy.

## 3. Sen house: clinic, school room, sleeping rooms

**Occupants.** Asha (healer, keeps the written decisions), Rian (sailmender and
shell worker, her younger brother).

- **Clinic.** Treat. A raised cot with a woven mat, shelves of corked jars sorted
  by colour, a washing table with a basin and a hanging towel, a lamp over the
  cot, a bench for waiting. Smells of sandalwood and boiled linen. *Only
  Asha's:* a roll of wrapped needles; a bundle of cuttings drying from the rafters;
  a stool worn smooth where she sits for the long cases.
- **School room.** Learn. A low table with three stools (Tavi's has his floats
  under it), a slate wall of chalk marks, a window onto the porch, a shelf of
  shells used for counting, a dry mat. *Only Asha's class:* Pree's drawing of a
  water bug taped to the wall; a row of wooden sailing boats the children carved.
- **Family entry and records room** (the house's private door). Asha's record
  shelves with tied bundles, the village's decisions in pictured knots on a
  cord board, a low bench, boots and oilskins on pegs, a lamp.
- **Kitchen and wash.** A clay brazier under its hood, a water jar, a basin
  and towel, a woodbox, a shelf of bowls for two.
- **Asha's room.** A bed, a chest, a lamp, a writing desk by the window.
- **Rian's room.** A narrow bed, sail repair needles in a roll, spools of thread,
  a drying line of buttons, a mother-of-pearl button on a nail by the door.
  Spare coils of rope in the corridor.

## 4. Rian's shell-works barge

**Occupant.** Rian. Work. A heavy bench with a vice, a foot-powered saw, a sealing
press, bins of shell sorted by thickness, a sailmaker's palm and needles on a
hook, a rope coil, a lamp swinging from the roof. *Only Rian's:* a pile of
discarded buttons he cannot bring himself to throw out; a notched post
counting seasons.

## 5. Cistern house and shared stores

**Occupant.** Leena keeps the stores. Store and draw. Shelves of rope, pitch, lamp
oil in jars, dried food in lidded baskets, a stack of spare lamps, a tap and
bucket ledge outside. *Only Leena's:* a ledger board with beads on a wire for
each household's share; a stool by the door where she checks the jars in the
evening.

## 6. Net shed and gear loft

**Occupants.** Salim, Jori (shared). Nets hang from the rafters like curtains,
floats in baskets, a bench for mending with a netting needle left in a half-tied
mesh, a barrel of tar, a ladder-ramp to the loft where traps and floats are
stacked. *Only Salim's:* a tied-off lucky knot over the door; a sleeping cat in
a basket.

## 7. Communal pavilion

**Occupant.** Leena keeps it. Meet, eat, mend, muster. Long tables and benches on
the north half, a clay stove with a hood for the shared meals, a bell on its post,
a repair bench with a vice at the east end, rainwater jars in the corners,
lamps hung in a row under the ridge. *Only the village's:* mugs that belong to
each household, hung on their own pegs in their own colours; a child's height
marks cut into a post.

## 8. Aran house and pearl yard

**Occupants.** Mai (pearl farmer), Salim (net worker, Mai's husband), Dala
(Mai's mother, weather watcher), Pree (Mai's daughter, herbs and water).

- **Veranda.** Watch. A chair with a worn seat for Dala, a barometer of shells on
  a string, a bell to call the weather, a rail with Pree's herb floats tied on
  short lines. A lamp on a hook and a blanket over the rail.
- **Living room.** Gather. A low table, cushions, a shelf of Dala's wooden
  charms, a bamboo flute on a nail, boards of different ages in the floor.
- **Kitchen.** A clay brazier with a hood, a rack of dried fish, jars of pickled
  weed, a stone mortar.
- **Mai and Salim's room.** A broad bed, a chest with Mai's grading tools, nets
  folded on a bench.
- **Dala's room.** A narrow bed by the window onto the water, a wall of charms
  and a rain-gauge jar, a chair she watches the lake from at night.
- **Pree's corner.** A mat bed under the stair, jars of living things (a snail,
  a water beetle), her drawings, a magnifying lens made from a pearl blank.

## 9. Pearl yard

**Occupant.** Mai. Grade. Three grading tables under shade, trays by size and
colour, a scale, a magnifier, basket racks of mussels, a fresh-water rinse jar,
a drying frame for shell. *Only Mai's:* a tray of pearls she will not sell, kept
under a cloth.

## 10. Vale family houseboat

**Occupants.** Jori (fisher, watches the boats come in), Osei (smokes and
processes the catch), Tavi (their son, 9).

- **Living room and galley.** A low table, mats, a brazier with a hood, rope
  shelves, oilskins on pegs, a fish bone chart on a plank. *Only the Vales':*
  Jori's old paddle with the far shore carved on it; a jar of smoked fish for
  guests; a battered thermos.
- **Jori and Osei's cabin.** A shared bed, a chest, a lamp, a window onto Heron
  Rock, a rack of Osei's smoke tools.
- **Tavi's cabin.** A narrow bed, cork floats of every size strung across the
  ceiling, a shelf of mechanical bits he has taken apart, a stool, a lamp hung low.
- **Work deck.** Floats drying on the rails, a gear store with nets and a bait
  box, a rinse bucket, mooring lines coiled.

## 11. Catch deck, drying shelter and smokehouse

**Occupant.** Osei. A sorting table, a rinse trough, slatted racks of drying fish,
a stone hearth in the smokehouse with a short flue, dark smoke staining on every
beam, a bucket of brine, a knife roll. *Only Osei's:* his grandmother's smoking
recipe cut into a board by the hearth (symbols, not words).

## 12. Mor guest houseboat

**Occupants.** Leena (hostess), Ivo (ferryman), Sela (utility boat pilot).

- **Arrival deck.** Receive. Benches along the side rail, a rack for muddy
  shoes, a lamp hung from the beam, a sleeping cat on the hatch, a bell for the
  ferry. Teal trim, terracotta panels.
- **Common cabin and galley.** Eat and sit. A long low table with floor cushions,
  shelves of mismatched crockery from every port, a clay brazier with a hood, a
  dry pantry with lidded baskets, a window onto the deck. *Only Leena's:* a jar
  of buttons left by guests who never came back; a seat by the window that nobody
  else sits in.
- **Party dormitory.** Sleep. Four beds against the long walls (heads to the wall),
  a chest at the foot of each with its back toward the sleeper, hooks, a lamp, a
  rack for packs. Warm and a little crowded.
- **Guest cabins (two).** A bed, a chest, a window, a lamp.
- **Stern cabin.** The Mor family's: a bed, charts on a rolled rack, Ivo's coat,
  Sela's boat keys on a nail. A closing door.
- **Service wall.** A contained marine composting toilet and wash space against
  the outer wall of the south walkway: a seat over a lidded bin, a water jar and
  basin, a vent stack through the roof. Never discharges into the lake.
- **Cargo deck.** Crates, a cargo winch, mooring cleats, a hand cart.

## 13. Ivo's ferry launch

Cargo well with crates marked by household colour, a cab with a chart roll and a
brass compass, a tin of biscuits, a coat on a hook, a lamp.

## 14. Arrival landing, Heron landing, portal landing

- **Arrival landing.** The market shelter's notice board, a bench, a stack of
  crates beside the ferry berth, coiled lines, a lamp on a post at each corner.
- **Heron landing.** A bench for watching boats, a mooring post with Jori's lamp.
- **Portal landing.** Bare deck, a lamp on each of two corner posts, a coil of
  rope. Nothing else.

## 15. Build order for the interiors

After the architecture is built and validated, furnish in this order: Venn house,
Sen house, Mor houseboat (the rest point), pavilion, Aran house, Vale houseboat,
the work decks, then the barge, launch and stores. Walk each at eye level.
