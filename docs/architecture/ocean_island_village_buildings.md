# Kai Mālie: Architecture Briefs

Status: building briefs for review (2026-10-06). Read with
`ocean_island_village.md` (community, charter, dress) and
`ocean_island_village_layout.md` (layout). Positions, footprints and owners
come from the layout.

## 1. Kit of parts

Two traditions, never mixed on one building: modern Hawaiian homes and shops
(about 70 percent) and traditional *hale* for the communal buildings (about
25 percent), with lava-rock walls as the accent.

### Invariants

| Element | Specification |
|---|---|
| Structural module | 3.0 m bays |
| Ground | every building stands on dark **lava-rock** piers or a lava-rock *paepae* (platform); the stone is the common thread |
| Palette | 60 percent painted board in soft greens, creams and warm reds, or weathered timber; 30 percent lava rock and thatch; 10 percent flowering plants and *kapa* patterns |
| Signature | the **lānai** (veranda): every house has one, on the side facing the green, the beach or the valley |
| Rainwater | gutters to a tank at each house's back corner |
| Light | lamps under eaves and on the *lānai*; no lights on poles |

### Modern Hawaiian homes and shops

| Element | Term | Specification |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation | post and pier | timber posts on lava-rock piers, floor 0.8 m above the ground, lattice skirting between the piers |
| Walls | single-wall board and batten | vertical boards with battens over the joints, painted |
| Roof | double-pitched hip | a steeper upper hip (38° to 42°) flaring to a shallower lower slope (18° to 22°) over the walls and *lānai*, as in Hawaiian regional architecture; eaves 1.2 m; corrugated metal or shingle |
| Windows | jalousie | louvred glass panes in superellipse frames, open for the trade wind |
| Doors | screen door over a panel door | the screen door the everyday entry |
| Access | ramp | a ramp to every *lānai* (no steps alone), 1.2 m wide, no steeper than 1:6 |

### Traditional *hale* (communal buildings)

| Element | Term | Specification |
|---|---|---|
| Base | *paepae* | a dry-laid lava-rock platform, 0.6 to 0.8 m high, with a ramp |
| Frame | timber posts and rafters | lashed joints, ridge pole |
| Roof | steep gable, thatched | *pili* grass thatch at 45° to 50°, low eaves |
| Walls | thatched or open | thatched end walls; long sides open or with low thatched walls |

### Exclusions

*Heiau* and any sacred platform or carving as decoration; tiki; resort and
hotel styles; plantation-manager mansions; lights on poles.

Sizes below are the building's walls and *lānai* together; the layout's
footprints add the 1.2 m eaves on every side (0.6 m on the *hale*), so every
building fits its approved footprint.

## 2. Building briefs

### *Hālau* (community; Puanani teaches, the kūpuna meet)

- *Hale*, 16 × 7.5 m, on a 0.8 m *paepae* at the head of the green.
- **Masses:** one long thatched house, six bays, its long side open to the green
  with a low thatched wall at the back.
- **Use:** a broad open floor for hula practice and lessons; the kūpuna's
  meeting mats at the upslope end; *kapa* hangings; *palapalai* fern and *kī*
  beds against the *paepae* (see the landscape plan).
- **Variation:** the largest *hale*; the village's front room.

### Kahananui home (Kawika, Malia, Keoni)

- 9 × 6 m, three bays including a 2 m *lānai* across the green-facing front;
  double-pitched hip.
- **Rooms:** living room; kitchen at the back; Kawika and Malia's bedroom;
  Keoni's room; a work porch at the side where Malia beats *kapa* and dries
  *lauhala*.
- **Colour:** soft green walls, cream trim.
- **History:** the oldest house, its lava-rock piers laid by Kawika's
  grandfather; the work porch added for Malia.
- **Variation:** the side work porch; the oldest, most weathered board.

### Kealoha home (Leilani, Makoa, Noelani)

- 9 × 5.5 m, three bays, double-pitched hip; *lānai* facing the green, back
  door to the *loʻi* path.
- **Rooms:** living room; kitchen with a poi-pounding board by the back door;
  Leilani and Makoa's bedroom; Noelani's room; a net store.
- **Colour:** cream walls, warm red trim.
- **Variation:** nets drying on the back rail; a surfboard under the *lānai*.

### Akana home (Puanani)

- 6 × 4.5 m, two bays, double-pitched hip; *lānai* facing the beach path.
- **Rooms:** living room with her chant books and an ipu (gourd drum) on a shelf;
  kitchen; bedroom.
- **Colour:** warm red walls, cream trim.
- **Variation:** the smallest house; the lei-making table on the *lānai*.

### Okafor guest house (Sam; rest point)

- 11 × 6.5 m, **two storeys**, double-pitched hip; a ground-floor *lānai* facing
  the pier head and a smaller upper *lānai*.
- **Ground floor:** reception and common room, a kitchen, Sam's room.
- **Upper floor:** three guest rooms and the party room where the hero wakes,
  reached by an interior ramp (5.2 m run, landings at both ends).
- **Colour:** soft green with cream trim.
- **History:** a plantation-era boarding house Sam bought and reopened.
- **Variation:** the only two-storey house besides the store; a hand-painted
  welcome board of a turtle and a wave (no words).

### Medeiros home and ukulele workshop (Manny, Kahala)

- 6 × 5.5 m home plus a 3.5 × 4 m workshop wing toward the green.
- **Rooms:** living room; kitchen; bedroom; Kahala's drying room for medicinal
  plants on the back; the workshop with a bench, bending iron and ukulele bodies
  hanging from the rafters.
- **Colour:** cream walls, soft green trim.
- **Variation:** the L plan; music on the *lānai* at dusk.

### Kahale home (Kahiau, Iolana)

- 6 × 4.5 m, two bays, double-pitched hip; *lānai* facing the canoe house.
- **Rooms:** living room with a star compass laid in shells on the floor; kitchen;
  two bedrooms.
- **Colour:** weathered timber, unpainted.
- **Variation:** the only unpainted house; adzes and paddles on the wall.

### Varga cottage (Elena)

- 4.5 × 3.5 m, one bay and a half, a simple hip; a small *lānai* toward the
  sea.
- **Rooms:** one room with a bed, a desk of specimen jars and reef charts, and a
  wet bench by the door.
- **Colour:** pale sea blue, the one cool colour in the village.
- **Variation:** the newest and smallest; wetsuit and reef boots on the rail.

### Nakamura store and shave ice, with the Nakamura home above (Hiro, Grace)

- 9 × 5.5 m, **two storeys**, double-pitched hip; a deep *lānai* along the pier
  head with the counter.
- **Ground floor:** the store (fruit for now, in crates and baskets), the shave
  ice counter with its ice shaver and bottles of colourful syrup, a storeroom.
- **Upper floor:** the Nakamura home: living room, kitchen, bedroom, reached by
  an outside ramp at the back.
- **Colour:** warm red walls, cream trim, a painted shave ice cone over the
  counter (no words).
- **History:** Hiro's grandfather's store.
- **Variation:** the shop front; the mango tree shading the counter.

### Canoe house (*hale waʻa*; Kahiau)

- *Hale*, 15 × 6 m, on a low lava-rock platform at the top of the beach, open on
  its seaward end.
- **Use:** two outrigger canoes on rests; paddles, lashing cord and adzes on the
  walls; *hau* booms and *koa*-coloured hulls under repair.
- **Variation:** the second *hale*; canoes sliding straight down to the water.

### Pier and pier head (public)

- 3.6 m wide timber pier on lava-rock-footed piles across the reef flat from the
  beach to the arrival dock; a widened pier head at the beach where the store,
  guest house and green meet.
- Rails on both sides; benches at the pier head.

### Fishpond wall (*loko kuapā*; Kawika)

- A curved dry-laid lava-rock wall about 2 m wide on top, enclosing about
  30 × 36 m of reef flat, with two *mākāhā* (sluice gates) of timber grills
  where young fish enter and grown fish cannot leave. The wall's crest is a
  2 m walking path.

### Footbridges, terrace walls and the *ʻauwai*

- Timber footbridges 2 m wide where paths cross the stream.
- Low dry-laid lava-rock walls holding each taro terrace; the *ʻauwai* lined with
  stone where it runs along the contour.

## Fixed, proposed and open

**Fixed:** the layout's positions, footprints and owners; the charter.

**Proposed:** the kit (lava-rock piers and *paepae*, board and batten,
double-pitched hip roofs, jalousies, *lānai*, thatched *hale*); every building's
form and rooms.

**Open:** none blocking.
