# Ocean Kingdom: The Cage Island

Status: concept brief, first planning pass, in review (2026-10-06). Read with
`ocean_kingdom.md`.

## 1. Premise

A smaller island holds a fighting cage and a population of small creatures
who live for the tournament. They make the hero fight their cage match
tournament, round by round, for their entertainment and the prizes.

## 2. Site

The proposed island is the low satellite at world `(112, 88)`, radius about
31 m, rising to about 3.8 m (`ocean_kingdom_terrain.gd`, `ISLANDS`). It lies
about 140 m east-northeast of the arrival dock, across open water from Kai
Mālie, so the cage is visible from the gate and from the village beach. It sits
inside the ships' route and well inside the Kraken's.

## 3. The inhabitants (proposed)

**The Shellbacks:** small hermit-crab people, about knee-high to the hero,
who live in found objects instead of shells: bottles, a ship's bell, a dented
helmet, a cooking pot, a lantern, a barrel stave curled around a body. They
scavenge everything that washes ashore or falls off a pirate ship, trade
homes as they grow, and judge one another by the quality of their shell.
They are loud, competitive and theatrical, and the tournament is their
culture's centre.

### Governance

Whoever holds the champion's title has the last word, which is why the title
matters so much. Clackjaw runs everything else.

## 4. The tournament

The hero fights contenders from every community in the kingdom. The Shellbacks
thrive on the Demon King's discord: each feuding community has sent a champion
to settle its scores in the cage, and the Shellbacks rank them all on one
ladder. Beating them is part of how the hero breaks the cycle of grievance,
though the reconciliation itself happens elsewhere.

### The ladder (proposed order, bottom to top)

| Rung | Contender | From |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Bottlecap** | the Shellbacks |
| 2 | **Cowrie** | the Shellbacks |
| 3 | **Makoa Kealoha**, the net fisher | Kai Mālie |
| 4 | **Driftnail** | the Shellbacks |
| 5 | **Tudaro**, the edge warden | the sea folk |
| 6 | **Yaw Mensah**, boatswain | the *Fortune's Rag* |
| 7 | **Old Kelp**, the *Harbinger*'s aged sailing master, the oldest pirate at sea and still the hardest to put down | the *Harbinger* |
| 8 | **Snapper** | the Shellbacks |
| 9 | **Old Ironshell**, reigning champion | the Shellbacks |

The sea folk contender fights in the water-filled half of the cage floor or in
a flooded variant of the cage; how a tailed fighter fights on sand is part of
the fight design.
- **Prizes:** shiny salvage, gear and the champion's title. Exact rewards are
  open.
- **Fights** use the game's existing combat. Whether the hero fights alone,
  with the party, or through bonded blorbs is open.

## 5. Settlement structure and architecture (pre-layout)

- **The cage:** an octagonal cage at the island's high point, about 18 m
  across, built from salvaged spars lashed with rope and chain and walled in
  net. One gate, guarded by Pinch. The cage floor is packed sand ringed by
  timber.
- **The stands:** tiered seating of crates, barrels and old hull planks around
  three sides, at Shellback scale, with a raised booth for Clackjaw over the
  gate and Tally's board of rankings beside it (pictures and marks, never
  words).
- **Shell homes:** the Shellbacks' found-object shells lie along the
  shoreline when not worn, with a few larger salvaged structures (an upturned
  rowboat, a ship's lantern room, a barrel stack) used as shared shelters.
- **The landing:** a beach on the side facing the arrival dock, littered with
  wreck timber.
- **Scale:** the Shellbacks are small, so their homes are too small to enter,
  but every public route, the stands' aisles and the cage gate meet the hero's
  and party's minimums (3 m routes, 3.2 m headroom).
- **Charter:** salvage vernacular. Everything is visibly something else first
  (a spar, a hatch cover, a net, a figurehead), lashed rather than built, in
  weathered timber, rope, rusted iron and brass, with the bright accent of
  painted pirate junk. No new-built carpentry.

## Fixed, proposed and open

**Fixed (from direction):** a smaller island with a battle cage; small creature
inhabitants who make the hero fight a cage match tournament; contenders from
every community, including an old pirate.

**Proposed:** the island at `(112, 88)`; the Shellbacks and their found-object
shells; the census of eleven; the ladder's order and its contenders, with Old
Kelp as the old pirate; the salvage
architecture.

**Open:** the species, if hermit-crab people are not right; how fights work and
who fights; the prizes; whether the champion title carries any power beyond
the island.
