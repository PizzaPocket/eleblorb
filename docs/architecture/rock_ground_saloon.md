# Rock and Ground: the Saloon and Hotel (architecture brief)

Status: building brief for review (2026-10-12). Read with
`rock_ground_town.md`: community (3), the mechanical and motor layer (2a), the
architecture direction (6), and the town layout concept (6a). The town's style
charter is still a direction, not an approved charter. This brief applies that
direction and flags where it fixes something the charter must then confirm.

## 1. People and purposes

| Who | What they do here |
|---|---|
| **Dolma Hearthstone** | Keeps the saloon and the hotel alone: she tends the bar, cooks, keeps the desk and the books, and lives upstairs. The plan lets one person work the bar, the desk and the kitchen without crossing the barroom. |
| **The party** | Arrives, rests (the town's rest point, 20 Tokoins a night), and wakes in the party room. It may be a large party: the hero, companions and mounts. |
| **Jessup Kane and Sadie Vex**, the gang | Lounge on the porch and the gallery all day, watching the square. They rent the big back room by the week and pay late. |
| **Townsfolk** | Drink, play cards, gather in the evening; Lark's racers celebrate here; Hazel, Gus and Cass come in from the basin and the mine. |
| **Travellers** | Take the corner room. |
| **Deliveries** | Walt's goods, barrels and ice come to the back door from the back lane. |

Through the day: quiet in the morning (Dolma cooks, sweeps, does the books);
the gang on the porch from noon; busy from dusk, with the player piano, cards
and the racers; the hotel quiet after midnight.

## 2. Site

- **The plot:** the north side of the well square, at the middle of main street
  (`rock_ground_town.md` 6a). The front faces south onto the square and the
  through road.
- **Kingdom coordinates (provisional until the measured layout):**
  - front wall at z ≈ 81, with the porch from z 81 to 84 on the square;
  - back wall at z ≈ 61.8;
  - x from −166.2 to −143.8;
  - a service yard behind to the back lane at z ≈ 54.
- **Ground:** the town shelf is a terrain pad at 8.0 m, flat. The ground floor
  stands on a 0.5 m stone plinth (floor 8.5 m). The porch deck is level with
  the north boardwalk, at 8.45 m.
- **Neighbours:** keep 4 m clear to the eave on both sides. The jail stands to
  the west. The house plot drawn to the east in the arrangement sketch must
  move about 0.5 m east to keep that 4 m.

## 3. Size: why it is the town's biggest building

The saloon is the social centre, the rest point and the gang's stage, so it
is the largest building in town and has the tallest front on the street.

| | Saloon | Holt Inn (Ohio), for comparison |
|---|---|---|
| Footprint | **22.4 × 19.2 m** (7 × 6 cells), 430 m² | 19.2 × 16 m, 307 m² |
| Storeys | two, with a double-height barroom | two, with a double-height hearth hall |
| Main room | barroom **16 × 12.8 m**, 205 m², double height to the roof trusses | common room |
| Seats | about 60: six card tables, the bar and the stage benches | |
| Front | two-storey cast-iron gallery across all 22.4 m | timber porch |

The program adds up along both axes (section 5), and every room is at or
above its minimum.

## 6. Exterior and style audit

**Facade (south, onto the square):**
- **Base:** a stone plinth, and a stone masonry ground storey on the side
  walls, after the Old Stone Fort. The front is brick, with tall doors and
  windows behind the gallery.
- **Middle:** the two-storey **cast-iron gallery** of Jefferson's commercial
  blocks across the whole front:
  - slender square iron columns at each 3.2 m cell line, never in front of a
    doorway;
  - an iron lace railing on the balcony;
  - a shed roof.
- **Top:** the stepped brick parapet with a corbelled cornice.

**Entrances, by purpose:**
- the barroom's tall double doors with batwings inside, centred, the focal
  point;
- the hotel's own door with a **Greek Revival door case** (square pilasters, a
  plain entablature): the building's one polite element, marking the hotel as
  the respectable half;
- a service door and double door at the back.

**Openings:**
- tall narrow windows with superellipse heads in the charter's proportion,
  framed on both faces;
- no shutters (a commercial building);
- tall windows on the east wall light the barroom.

**On the porch:**
- hitching rails and bike stands at the kerb, with one charging post;
- a bench where the gang sits;
- pictures-only signs: a painted glass and a horse's head on the parapet
  board, with no lettering.

| Element | Term | Tradition |
|---|---|---|
| Two-storey iron gallery with lace railing | cast-iron gallery, cresting | Texas frontier commercial (Jefferson) |
| Stepped brick front hiding the roof | parapet, corbelled cornice | Texas frontier commercial |
| Stone side walls | coursed rubble masonry | Texas frontier commercial (Old Stone Fort) |
| Low gable behind the parapet | gable roof | Texas frontier commercial |
| Hotel door | Greek Revival door case | Greek Revival (secondary) |
| Batwing doors on spring hinges | café doors | frontier saloon; mechanical layer |
| Exposed trusses with iron rods, belt-driven fans, knife switches, player piano | Howe truss, line shaft | mechanical and motor accent |

Two traditions plus the accent: within the rule.

## 7. Services

- **Heat:** one brick stack in the back cross-wall:
  - the barroom's cast-iron stove in its breast;
  - the kitchen range on its back;
  - its flue warming the gang's room above.

  Desert nights are cold, and this is the building's heart.
- **Water:** from the town's water tower across the square, by a buried main.
  It feeds the kitchen, both washrooms and an outside tap in the yard.
- **Toilets (the rest point's):**
  - **Downstairs:** two porcelain flush toilets with cisterns, in closets with
    hung doors, plus basins.
  - **Upstairs:** a toilet, a basin and a shower for guests.

  The town is layered present-day, so the fixtures are modern. Waste goes to
  a septic tank and soakaway in the service yard, never to open ground near
  the well.
- **Power and the mechanical layer:**
  - electric bulbs from the town dynamo, switched at the office's knife-switch
    board;
  - ceiling fans on an exposed belt line from a small motor on the barroom's
    back wall;
  - the player piano;
  - a case clock in the lobby;
  - spring-hinged batwings.

  Every one of them works (`rock_ground_town.md` 2a).
- **Air:** the barroom's high windows open; the kitchen vents through the back
  wall; the roof vents at the back gable.

## 8. Traversal and gameplay

- **Routes:**
  - square → porch → batwings → bar, stove and stage;
  - square → hotel door → lobby → ramp → landing hall → party room;
  - lobby ↔ barroom by the archway;
  - corridor to every room;
  - balcony from the party room and the west gallery.
- **Widths:** the ramp is 2.0 m, the corridor and galleries 1.4 m, the party
  room's door 2.0 m, and the barroom and hotel front doors 2.4 m. Walk all of
  them at human, Blorbus and mount scale. The wide ramp and party-room door
  are for big companions.
- **The porch:** at boardwalk level, with ramps of 1 in 8 at both ends and a
  broad ramp at the centre from the square. No steps.
- **The rest point:** the party room is the wake room. Dolma is at the desk or
  the bar according to the hour. 20 Tokoins a night (unchanged).
- **Collision:**
  - walls, plinth, gallery columns, railings, bar, stage, stove breast, tables
    and the piano are blocking;
  - the balcony rail top and the parapet cornice are parkour landings; a
    player can climb the gallery to the balcony;
  - bottles and glasses are decorative.
- **No text:** signs are pictures only.

## 10. Beds at the world's scale (2026-10-12)

The party room's bed follows the world rule now written into the
architecture skill.
- **Mattress length:** at least the tallest likely sleeper plus 0.2 m.
- **Who that is:** the game's people stand about 2.0 m × their body scale.
  The hero is about 1.9 m, and men run up to about 2.3 m.
- **Sizes:**
  - a double is 1.6 × 2.5 m (frame about 1.75 × 2.65);
  - a single is 1.05 × 2.5 m.

The shared bed today is 1.32 × 2.16 m, too short for most of the town's men.

## 9. Fixed, proposed and open

**Fixed:**
- the saloon is the town's inn, its social centre and rest point (user,
  2026-10-07);
- it stands on the through road, not blocking it (user, 2026-10-12);
- Dolma Hearthstone keeps it, with the gang on the porch;
- a modern toilet;
- no firearms anywhere;
- it must be big enough (user, 2026-10-12): it is the largest building in
  town.
