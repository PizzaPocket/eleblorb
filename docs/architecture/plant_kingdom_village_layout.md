# Plant Kingdom: Primate Village Layout

Status: dimensioned schematic for review (2026-10-06). Read with
`plant_kingdom_village.md` (community and charter). Local frame: origin at the
village centre (the kingdom origin); `x` and `z` follow world axes, `+z` toward
the river.

## 1. Survey details that drive the plan

- **Flat ground** within 55 m of the centre, blending over 22 m more; jungle
  scatter cleared only within 24 m. The plan uses the whole flat disc and asks
  the foliage clear radius to grow to 60 m.
- **Gate** at `(0, -3)`, arrivals at `(0, 4)`: the village's centre is its
  arrival point.
- **Giant trees** at `(14, 12)` (56 m), `(-16, 9)` (62 m) and `(2, -18)` (58 m).
  Trunk radius at the base is about 0.045 of the height (2.5 to 2.8 m). Branch
  ramps rise **3.8 m over a 3.6 m run, about 46°**, 1.5 m wide, with a landing
  at every rise. The canopy spreads about 19 to 21 m from each trunk.
- **The ramps are much steeper than the world's 32° rule.** They work as
  climbing for monkeys but are hard on the party. The plan keeps them as the
  climbing route and adds a gentler public route (section 4).
- **River** about 150 m to the south, water at -3 m.

## 2. Plan

The village has three layers: a small camp on the ground, a large shared
**commons** deck at 11.4 m spanning the three trees above the gate plaza, and
the households' rings and bridges higher in the trees.

## 3. The commons (11.4 m)

A large shared deck lashed between the three trunks at their 11.4 m landings,
where canopy and floor households meet. It is the village's centre: elevated,
reachable by everyone, and directly above the arrival.

### Structure

- **Shape:** a rounded triangle about 30 m across, its three corners wrapping
  the three trunks; about 350 m² of deck.
- **Support:** tie beams and vine cables to each trunk, cable stays from higher
  on the trunks, and six timber posts down to the ground placed outside the
  gate plaza's clear zone, so the gate and arrival stay open below with about
  11 m of headroom and shade.
- **Edges:** rope rails all round, with **catch nets** slung beneath the edges,
  as monkeys would.
- **Roofs:** several broad leaf roofs on posts, in the treehouse style, over the
  kitchen, dining terrace and market; the centre stays open to the sky.

### Shared amenities

| Amenity | Keeper | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Gathering circle | the elders; Purnama presides | an open ring of log seats at the centre; village councils, stories and the evening gathering. The elders come down to it, so the floor households are heard |
| Shared kitchen | Wangi | the **one fire in the trees**: a clay-and-stone hearth on a thick stone-lined base set in a sand bed, water gourds beside it, smoke escaping through a vent in its leaf roof |
| Dining terrace | everyone | long low tables beside the kitchen |
| Market | Delima's fruit stall; Rotan's rope and vine | stalls under a long leaf roof facing the grand ramp's arrival |
| Rain cistern and washing | shared | the largest leaf roof funnels rain into a lashed barrel cistern; washing basins below it |
| Food store | shared | hanging baskets and a raised store out of reach of ground pests |
| Inn (proposed move) | Bima | the inn moves up from the ground to the commons' edge beside the East Tree, same price |

### Getting up

- **Grand ramp:** a broad switchback ramp, 2.4 m wide, no steeper than 32°,
  rising from the gate plaza between the Elder and East trees to the commons'
  southern edge (about 18 m of run in three flights with landings).
- **Tree switchbacks:** each tree's 32° public switchback reaches the commons at
  its 11.4 m corner, then continues to the higher rings.
- **Climbing:** the trees' steep branch ramps and rope ladders, for climbers.

## 5. The upper canopy

### Household rings

Each tree carries **wrap-around decks** (rings) lashed to the trunk on knee
braces and vine cables, replacing the scattered 3.6 m decks. A ring is about 5 m
wide. On each ring stand **open pavilions** in the existing treehouse style (a
central timber pillar, open sides, a broad leaf roof), with hammock nests,
storage baskets and rain gourds.

| Tree | Rings above the commons | Households |
|---|---|---|
| Elder Tree (62 m) | 22.8 m (bridge level), 34.2 m, and the elders' crown deck at 45.6 m | Purnama at the crown; Abu, Senja, Rimba and Sekar below |
| East Tree (56 m) | 22.8 m (bridge level), 34.2 m | Dahan, Embun, Bayu and Tunas; Jati's workshop on the commons corner |
| North Tree (58 m) | 22.8 m (bridge level), 34.2 m | Tirta, Bintang, Intan and Murai |

Heights match the existing landing spacing (every 3.8 m).

### Bridges

Rope-and-plank bridges join the three trees at 22.8 m, spanning about 30 to
32 m with a gentle sag, 2 m wide, with rope rails, so the households above the
commons are one neighbourhood.

## 6. Systems

- **Water:** leaf roofs drain into gourds and bamboo tubes on the rings, and into
  the commons cistern; the river for fishing and washing.
- **Food:** foraged fruit to the market and the shared kitchen; fish from the
  river.
- **Fire:** only the shared kitchen's stone-based hearth on the commons; none on
  the rings.
- **Light:** enclosed lamps of glowing fruit rind or resin on the rings and the
  commons.
- **Safety:** rails and catch nets on the commons; the safe zone covers the flat
  disc; ape skeleton NMEs stay outside it.
- **Performance:** no more residents than today; fewer, larger structures.

## 7. Schedule anchors

By day: canopy residents on their rings; Delima and Rotan at the market; Wangi in
the kitchen; Melati in the gardens; Ossian with Manchego at the stable; Batu at
the training grounds; Sari at the tree line; Tirta at the river in the
evening. At dusk everyone gathers on the commons to eat, and the elders sit in
the gathering circle. Bima at the inn. No two residents share a spot.

## Fixed, proposed and open

**Fixed:** the three trees and their positions, the gate, the inn's keeper and
price, Ossian and Manchego, the open treehouse character.

**Proposed:** the commons at 11.4 m with its shared amenities, grand ramp,
catch nets and the one fire in the trees; the gate plaza beneath it; the ground
camp with a stable, paddock and gardens; household rings above the commons;
bridges at 22.8 m; a 32° switchback on each tree; the inn's move to the
commons; the training grounds' place; the river trail; the wider foliage
clearing.

**Open:** none blocking.
