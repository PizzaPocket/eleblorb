# Plant Kingdom: Primate Village Layout

Status: dimensioned schematic for review (2026-10-06). Read with
`plant_kingdom_village.md` (community and charter). Local frame: origin at the
village centre (the kingdom origin); `x` and `z` follow world axes, `+z` toward
the river.

## 1. Survey details that drive the plan

- **Flat ground** within 55 m of the centre, blending over 22 m more; jungle
  scatter cleared only within 24 m. The plan uses the whole flat disc and asks
  the foliage clear radius to grow to 60 m.
- **Gate** at `(0, -3)`, arrivals at `(0, 4)`: the village's centre is its
  arrival point.
- **Giant trees** at `(14, 12)` (56 m), `(-16, 9)` (62 m) and `(2, -18)` (58 m).
  Trunk radius at the base is about 0.045 of the height (2.5 to 2.8 m). Branch
  ramps rise **3.8 m over a 3.6 m run, about 46°**, 1.5 m wide, with a landing
  at every rise. The canopy spreads about 19 to 21 m from each trunk.
- **The ramps are much steeper than the world's 32° rule.** They work as
  climbing for monkeys but are hard on the party. The plan keeps them as the
  climbing route and adds a gentler public route (section 4).
- **River** about 150 m to the south, water at -3 m.

## 4. The canopy

### Household rings

Each tree carries **wrap-around decks** (rings) lashed to the trunk on knee
braces and vine cables, replacing the scattered 3.6 m decks. A ring is about 5 m
wide around the trunk. On each ring stand **open pavilions** in the existing
treehouse style (a central timber pillar, open sides, a broad leaf roof), with
hammock nests, storage baskets and rain gourds.

| Tree | Ring heights (landings) | Households |
|---|---|---|
| Elder Tree (62 m) | 11.4 m, 22.8 m (bridge level), 34.2 m, and the elders' crown deck at 45.6 m | Elden Barrow at the crown; Corvin, Sable, Yarrow and Wisha on the rings below |
| East Tree (56 m) | 11.4 m, 22.8 m (bridge level), 34.2 m | Rook's workshop ring at 11.4 m; Kesh, Marlow, Bracken and Nettle above (the loose third landing stays loose) |
| North Tree (58 m) | 11.4 m, 22.8 m (bridge level), 34.2 m | Tamsin, Tovik, Pemberly and Linnet |

Heights match the existing landing spacing (every 3.8 m), so the rings sit on
real landings.

### Bridges

Rope-and-plank **bridges** join the three trees at the 22.8 m rings, spanning
about 30 to 32 m with a gentle sag, 2 m wide, with rope rails at both sides.
They form a triangle, so the canopy works as one neighbourhood.

### Getting up

- **Climbing route:** the existing branch ramps, steep and fun, for climbers.
- **Public route:** one **switchback ramp** per tree on its plaza side, no
  steeper than 32°, 1.5 m wide with rails, rising from the ground to the first
  ring, then from ring to ring, so Blorbus and the party can reach every ring
  and bridge.

## 5. Systems

- **Water:** leaf roofs drain into hanging gourds and bamboo tubes; the river for
  washing and fishing.
- **Food:** foraged fruit to the stall and the hearth; fish from the river;
  cooked on the ground.
- **Light:** enclosed lamps of glowing fruit rind or resin on the rings; the
  hearth's fire on the ground.
- **Safety:** the safe zone covers the flat disc and its transition; ape skeleton
  NMEs stay outside it.
- **Performance:** no more residents than today; fewer, larger treehouse
  structures in place of many small ones; the foliage clear radius widened only
  to the village's edge.

## 6. Schedule anchors

Canopy residents work on their rings by day, gather at the hearth at dusk and
climb home at night. Sedge at the stall by morning; Ilva in the gardens; Tamsin
at the river in the evening; Torvin at the training grounds; Maddox at the tree
line watching for Kova Kong; Ossian and Manchego at the hitch; Bima at the inn;
Elden on the crown deck. No two residents share a spot.

## Fixed, proposed and open

**Fixed:** the three trees and their positions, the gate, the inn, Ossian and
Manchego, the open treehouse character.

**Proposed:** the gate plaza; ground buildings for every floor household; the
hearth; the fruit stall; household rings at real landing heights; bridges at
22.8 m; a 32° switchback public route on each tree; the training grounds' place;
the river trail; the wider foliage clearing.

**Open:** none blocking.
