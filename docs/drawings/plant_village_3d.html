What building the model turned up

The village layout is now data with checks (tools/plant_village_layout.py), and the model is drawn from it. Placing everything at true size exposed these.

Brief error

The commons cannot be 520 m². The triangle between the three trunk centres is already 432 m². A deck that wraps the trunks with 3.5 m to spare is about 800 m², 660 m² once the climbing wells are cut out round the trunks. That is still more room than the program needs, so the amenities fit with routes between them.

Brief error

The rope bridges span 12 to 14.5 m, not 30 to 32 m. The trunks stand 30 to 34 m apart, but each household ring reaches 9 m out from its trunk (a 2 m well, then 5 m of ring, then the homes' bays), so the bridges only cross the gap between rings.

Resolved

How people go up. The briefs had three routes in conflict:

  • the trees' old steep branch ramps, a wandering chain whose landings stand 3 to 9 m from the trunk, cutting through the commons, the rings and the homes;
  • a 32° switchback tower beside each trunk, which took the commons' best ground and crowded the bridges;
  • rope ladders.

The plan now gives every trunk two spirals and a well:

  • The climbers' spiral. Every deck stands back 2 m from its trunk, leaving an open well. In it the trees' own 46° ramps climb as a tight spiral, from the ground to the canopy, with a short arm across the well to each deck. It is the canopy monkeys' fast way up, and the trees' signature.
  • The public spiral. Just outside the well, under the rings, a 32° ramp 1.5 m wide winds from the commons to every ring and to the elders' crown. It comes up through an opening in each ring. The hero, the party, visitors and the old use it.
  • The hoist. A basket on a rope lifts food, water and firewood from the floor to the food store, so nobody carries loads up the ramps.

The switchback towers and the rope ladders are gone.

Placed

The brief gave no positions for the amenities on the commons. They now stand along the deck's edges with 1.5 m routes between them:

  • the market on the south edge, facing the grand ramp's head;
  • the kitchen and dining terrace on the north-west edge;
  • the store, cistern and Jati's workshop on the north-east edge, with the public privy hung just outside the rail beside the store;
  • the gathering circle, 7.2 m across, at the middle;
  • the inn on a lobe of deck that reaches round the East Tree's well.

The six posts stand 1 m inside the deck edge, all outside the gate plaza.

Built today

The game still has the prototype village:

  • the three giant trees with their steep branch ramps;
  • a 3.6 m treehouse deck on every fourth landing;
  • the inn on the ground at (27, 22).

None of the commons, rings, bridges, ground camp or switchbacks exist yet. The trees' branch ramps climb at 46°, and the model draws their chain from the same rule as nature_props.gd. Their starting directions are random in the game, so the model's are approximate.

Approximate

The terrain's hills and the river's wander come from Godot noise that the page cannot reproduce exactly. Their sizes match the code; their exact shapes do not. The village clearing, the river's depth and level, and every landmark position are exact.
