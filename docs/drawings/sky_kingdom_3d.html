What building the model turned up

The courts' layout is now data with checks (tools/sky_kingdom_layout.py), and the model is drawn from it. Placing every building with its base platform and ramp, and walking a 3 m route from each forecourt to every door, exposed these in the approved layout.

Resolved

Base platforms and ramps running into each other. The approved footprints were drawn without their base platforms or ramps:

  • Aethra: the Hall of Mist's three-tier base came within 0.3 m of Mnesia's tholos and of Aristeon's. Mnesia's tholos moves 6 m west, to (−20, −6). Aristeon's turns its ramp east toward the axis.
  • Koinon: the observatory's spiral ramp came within 1.3 m of the Hall of Assembly, and the archive's ramp ran into the hall. The cloud grows from 36 to 40 m. The observatory moves out 3 m, the archive 3 m west with its door turned to face the forecourt, and the philosopher's house 3 m east.
  • Pantao: the banquet hall's serving passage ran into the Peach Garden, and the kitchen stood 0.7 m from the hall. The hall moves 2 m forward and the garden back, at 26 × 18 m. The kitchen moves 4 m west.
  • Chrysa: the apprentice's bench stood 2 m from the forge's base; it moves 1 m east.
Resolved

Three things in the wrong place:

  • The judges' tholos stood on the racing track. Its base reached 34.6 m from Dromos's centre, and the track starts at 34 m. It moves 1.5 m in.
  • The herald's room was not moored. The brief moors it by a gold chain to a pier at the cloud's edge, its door open to the sky. The layout put it 21 m inside the cloud. It now hangs off Aethra's west rim, reached by a gangway from its pier.
  • The Dawn Gate cloud was too small. The gate, its ramp and a 12 m forecourt need a cloud 20 m across in radius, not 16.
Resolved

How people move inside a court. Every court now has a 3 m route from its landing forecourt to every door, kept off every base platform, ramp and building and 1.5 m in from the cloud's edge:

  • Aethra: every way in passes through the propylon, then branches from the inner court. The propylon loses its base platform, whose ramp took the forecourt. The axis's tier pines now flank the hall's ramp.
  • Koinon: routes run through the Hall of Assembly's ring colonnade, round the void. That is what the hall is for.
  • Routes round a building take up to two bends. The model draws them in gold.
Decide

From the Dawn Gate you cannot see the Hall of Mist. The layout's arrival view has Aethra "straight ahead and above", its hall on the axis. But Aethra's cloud is 28 m above the landing and its near rim is 47 m from the forecourt. The eye line to the top of the hall's roof passes about 5 m below that rim. From the gate you see the underside of a great cloud and nothing on it, and the gate's own roof cuts the view too. Three ways to answer it:

  • Terrace Aethra (recommended). Its cloud steps up toward the hall: the forecourt and propylon on a lower terrace at about +16 m, the hall's base at +28 m as now. From the gate the whole hall then stands in view above the lower terrace, and Aethra keeps its height and rank. It suits the stepped base the hall already has.
  • Lower Aethra to about +12 m. The hall's roof shows over the rim. But Aethra would no longer be the highest court unless every court comes down with it, into a 12 m band.
  • Keep it as a reveal. Aethra is a towering cloud from the gate, and the hall appears only as the hero flies up to it.

Whichever you choose, the gate's central bay frames Koinon, low and far beyond, not Aethra: Aethra is too high to fit in the bay.

Open

Two questions for you:

  • Which way the kingdom faces. The layout's frame points away from Ohio, but the staircase's direction is random today, so the courts' compass bearing changes every run. The nectar terraces must face world east. Proposed: fix the staircase on Ohio's north side and point the kingdom north, so world east is to the right of the arrival axis.
  • Dromos's track crossing. The landing forecourt is inside the racing ring, so a walker never needs to cross the track. Drop the crossing, or move the forecourt outside the ring so arrivals cross it at the judges' tholos.
Built today

The game has the prototype: four round cloud islands placed by offsets from the landing, with a round pavilion each and seeded spires, obelisks and dwellings. None of the six courts' buildings exist yet. The names are still the prototype's (Highcloud, Anvil Cloud, Drift Cloud, Hollow Cloud).
