What the chart turned up

Fixed

The cage island is east-south-east of the dock, not east-north-east as three documents said: it lies at z = +88, which is south. Corrected in the world bible, the kingdom overview and the cage island brief.

Resolved

The ships' route never came near the three wrecks they salvage every day: it ran 285 to 350 m out, the wrecks lie 184 to 202 m out. The ships now sail two nested loops in opposite directions, each past its own wreck and the contested galleon, and heave to over their wrecks at fixed hours (below). Kai Mālie's beach trade, after harmony, goes by boat from the inner loop.

Conflict

The luminous trail cannot bend round the Kraken's water: the Kraken circles the dock, so any trail from the dock to the city must cross its route once. It also runs over the coastal-forest islet's slope at (−34, −154).

Note

The main island's reshaping and the cage crater fit their sites: neither touches another islet, the reef flat ends just short of the dock, and the pier lands on it.
