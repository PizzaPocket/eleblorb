Reading the model

From

The plan file (tools/city_layout.py, which writes scripts/city_plan.gd): 25 streets, 6 squares and the park, 49 buildings and 194 lots, each at its planned height (a lot's storeys times 3.25 m, or 3.6 m on the mid-rise market streets). It is the same data as the Moy plan and the Grand Hotel model.

Rough
  • Every lot and ordinary building is a plain block at its storey count: no roofs, terraces, wind towers, balconies or signs.
  • The towers are drawn in their own forms only roughly: the Windspire's quarter-turn and wind gaps, the BLOCK tower's dark windowless shaft, the north tower's capsules and the south tower's rippling balconies.
  • The Grand Hotel is its Court, cylinder and drum; its own model has the detail.
Check

Meridian Avenue runs from city hall's front out to x = −340, about 20 m past the Rim Walk, which is 200 m from the city's centre. The avenue should stop at the Rim Walk, or the Rim Walk should open into an east gate there.

Not drawn
  • The Deck, the walkway two storeys up that links the landmarks, is described in the brief but has no route in the plan file yet, so it is not drawn.
  • The roof network, the swing corridors, the street trees and the people.
