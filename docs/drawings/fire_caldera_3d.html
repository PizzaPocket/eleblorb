Reading the model

From

Three plan files:

  • tools/fire_caldera_walkways_layout.py for every walkway above the lava and the funicular.
  • tools/fire_caldera_heartworks_layout.py for the chamber.
  • The Godot export of FireCalderaPlan for the ground and the buildings.

The two Python tools share tools/walkway_kit.py. It fuses each level into one surface, derives the guards from that surface's outline, and fails when a guard ends in mid-air, a flight's landing is not flush with its level, or a slope is too steep. The documents are fire_caldera_walkways.md and fire_caldera_heartworks.md.

The rules
  • One floor per level. A level is one surface at one height. A child walk joins its parent by becoming part of the same surface, so the floor runs through the joint without a seam.
  • Joints between levels. A ramp or stair starts and ends on a short level landing that belongs to the level it leaves. The level's outline turns along the landing and stops at a square mouth exactly as wide as the flight, where the flight's own guards begin.
  • Buildings. A building's walls are cut out of the level, so its porch and the walkway are one surface with one guard.
  • Corners. Concave corners are rounded at 1 m; mouths stay square.
  • Guards and kerbs. A guard stands only where the drop is a metre or more. Elsewhere, including along the lava, the edge is a kerb.
Rough
  • Buildings are their footprints at their planned heights; the guard's posts, glass and rail are drawn simply; stairs show their steps but not their stringers.
  • The ground is the export's 2 m grid, with the new reservoir bed (the dome) inside the bank.
