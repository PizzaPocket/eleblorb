Reading the model

From

Two plan files. Above the lava, FireCalderaPlan as built, exported by tools/validate_fire_plan.tscn -- --model-json: the ground, the reservoir's outline, the plots and every surface route at its real height. Below it, tools/fire_caldera_heartworks_layout.py, which writes scripts/fire_caldera_heartworks_plan.gd and fails when the section stops fitting together. The document is docs/architecture/fire_caldera_heartworks.md.

Changed

The first model's numbers could not all be true at once. This one starts again from its intent and keeps its sphere, rings, falls and rooms, but:

  • Its sphere's top stood 3.6 m above the lava, so the "submerged" throat was really the sphere's open top. The sphere now sits under a solid crown: its top is 14 m down, and the throat is a 17 m well in the crown, full of lava, floored by the sphere's cap.
  • Its Crown Gallery, 4 m down, was inside the lava. It is now a dry ring 10.5 m down, cut into the crown round the throat, under 1.5 m of roof. The reservoir's bed is now a 5 m shelf over it, not the old 22 m bowl.
  • Its rings were flat circles in the void. Each ring now follows the shell: the upper rings are galleries cut back into it (the shell overhangs them), the lower ones terraces standing out from it, every floor with at least 3 m of headroom.
  • Its falls dropped from nowhere onto nothing. Each fall now leaves a gated sluice in the throat's floor and lands in its own catch: the Heartfall in the basin, the Renewal Fall in the tempering pool of a shelf held out from the Steward Ring, the Forge Fall in a splitter bowl on an arm across the void, which channels a third of it to the forge. What the shelf and the splitter pass on falls as thin veils to the basin.
  • Its rooms floated in the void and its entries were lines on a map. The rooms are now dug into the rock behind their rings. Each lava entry is a real way: a shelf, a portal in the reservoir's bed, a pressure lock, a race well (a helical stair with a lava flume beside it) and a tunnel into its room.
  • The Civic relief riser stood toward the gate (278°); it now stands at 72°, and both relief vents run to the west and far shelves.
  • Rings are named C0 to C5 so they no longer share names with the Highline's routes (H0, H1, …).
Rough
  • Buildings are their footprints at their planned heights, not their architecture; the Highline's guards, mullions and edge pipes, and the Tempering Gate's form, are suggested only.
  • Tunnels, wells, stairs and risers are drawn as tubes on their centrelines; rooms as plain sectors.
  • The ground is the export's 2 m grid, so the throat's wall and the bank look softer than they are.
Check
  • The surface export is from before Kel's Highline ribbon was removed; the model leaves that route out. Re-export the surface with Godot to bring it level.
  • The live ground still digs the old 22 m reservoir bowl. Building the Heartworks means replacing it inside the bank with the shelf and the throat (bed_depth in the plan tool).
  • Emergency spoke E3 is at 155° in FireCalderaPlan, on the north-west, while the layout document calls it the east ascent from Kel and Oren. One of them needs to change.
