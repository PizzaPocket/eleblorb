Tennison Saloon and Hotel

The town's largest building, on the north side of the well square, facing the through road. It is 22.4 × 19.2 m (7 × 6 cells of 3.2 m) on two storeys. The barroom rises the full height to the roof trusses, and the hotel has its own door. Dolma Hearthstone keeps it all alone. The plans are drawn true scale with the street at the bottom and north at the top.

Footprint22.4 × 19.2 m, about 430 m² a floor (the Holt Inn is 19.2 × 16)
Barroom16 × 12.8 m, double height, about 60 seats
Rest pointparty room 4.2 × 11.4 m with one double bed (1.55 × 2.3 m mattress, the world's bed size), 2 m double door, 20 Tokoins
