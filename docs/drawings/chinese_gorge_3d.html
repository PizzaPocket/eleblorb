The Abyss as a Gorge of Pillars

The village's islands as what they would be in nature: the stone pillars left standing when water worked down the joints of a sandstone plateau. The same process made Zhangjiajie's pillars and the great tiankeng sinkholes. They stand in rows along the joints, their blocky tops at gently different heights. Small bare pillars with pines crowd between them. Mist fills the Abyss below the village, so the pillars still seem to float. Drag to look around, scroll to zoom, and pick a place to visit.

Why it read as unnatural, and what changes

Today

What the game builds now (switch to Built today to see it):

  • The Abyss is a perfect circle, 170 m across in radius, with smooth walls 400 m deep.
  • The islands are round grass discs over cones of rock, every one at the same height, set evenly in a ring round the palace.
  • Nothing holds them up, and nothing explains where they came from.
  • The bridges are flat planks 42 to 77 m long with nothing under them.
Proposed

The same village on the ground nature would give it:

  • A gorge, not a pit. It is about 450 × 560 m, long along its main joint (15° east of north), with a bay to the west, narrowing south to the entry from Ohio. The walls are sheer, banded sandstone.
  • Pillars, not discs. Each island is a blocky rounded-corner rectangle turned with the joints, with a rough edge and here and there a notch where a joint bit in. Every one stands on the floor 400 m down.
  • Rows along the joints. The pillars stand close across the gorge and wider along it, the way joint sets open.
  • Accordant tops. The tops are what is left of the old plateau surface, which tilted gently down to the south. The palace's pillar, the biggest remnant, stands highest at +4 m. The gate's stands lowest at −2.2 m. Every bridge climbs no more than 1 in 8.
  • Bare pillars. Twenty pillars too small to live on, 12 to 28 m across, stand between the lived-on ones at heights from 35 m below the village to 26 m above it. Cloud-pruned pines grow on their tops. They are what makes it read as a real stone forest rather than a set of plates.
  • Mist below. Mist fills the Abyss from about 60 m under the village. Look across and the pillars float. Look down and they plunge into it.
Kept

The village plan carries over:

  • The re-fit's land: every lived-on pillar's top is at least the area its program needs (chinese_village.md 4.1.2).
  • The roles and households: palace, gate, inn, roofers, Lantern Row with Sun Wu Kong's rock, market, fields and the farm.
  • The ring and the processional axis. Two small pillars now carry bridge-heads, one on the axis and one on the west ring, so the longest bridges drop from 77 m to 59 m. Every span is checked against its type: iron chain up to 100 m, covered timber cantilever up to 38 m, stone arch up to 15 m.
Decide

Three changes for you:

  • Pillars instead of floating islands. The world bible says the islands float. Here they stand on the Abyss's floor and only seem to float above the mist. That keeps the look while making the place natural.
  • Tops at different heights. The village code assumes one surface height for every island, for NPCs, Pandy and Sun Wu Kong. Pillars at heights from −2.2 to +4 m need a height per island.
  • The Abyss's shape. The terrain's circular pit becomes this gorge outline, with the same 400 m depth.
