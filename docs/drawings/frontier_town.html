Tennison Town Plan

A Western main street in the manner of Nacogdoches and Jefferson, set on a level shelf in the Rock half of a 1,240 m kingdom. The look is cowboy, mechanical and motor punk: cyborg horses that become dirt bikes, clockwork hats, windpumps and open machinery. The kingdom map is true scale and taken from the terrain code. The town arrangement below it is a first sketch of the brief's places list; it is not yet a measured layout.
