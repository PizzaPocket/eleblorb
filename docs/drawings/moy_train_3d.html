The Moy Car

One short car for every line in the city, built entirely of SuperEggs like everything else in the world: a soft-cornered cream body, a dark window band wrapping round both ends, a band in the line's colour, the roof battery that the wind charges, and two bogies. Trains are two or three cars long.

    The car's parts

    Each part is one SuperEgg as scripts/superegg.gd builds it: semi-axes along the car, up and across, and two exponents, the first for the plan shape and the upper half, the second for the lower half. 2 is a plain ellipse; the higher the number, the boxier the egg.
