How the bridges stand

Iron chain bridge · 41 to 77 m

After the Luding Bridge (1706). Chains run into stone bridge-head houses that anchor them and double as gate and lamp station. Chain handrails are at 1.05 m with a continuous guard collider.

Covered cantilever · 26 to 37 m

Logs step out from each abutment, with their tails weighted under a masonry house. A short beam closes the middle. A tiled gallery keeps the timber dry, and benches line the sides.

Stone arch · 11.5 m

One segmental arch springing from the rock lips of the fields island and Tian Bo's farm. Classical humpbacks have steps; this one has a ramp.
