Sea Folk City in Depth

Two cities, one over the other, 540 m north of the arrival dock. The classical city is preserved on the seabed, 72 m down. The modern city hangs in the water above it, because the sea folk swim in three dimensions and are not bound to the floor. Every form is a SuperEgg: square-ended and grounded for the old city, round-ended and buoyant for the new. Drag to look around, scroll or pinch to move closer, or pick a place to be taken there.

Half-section, centre to edge

From Tidehall at the centre out to the city's edge, at true heights and radii. The classical city keeps the floor. The modern city fills the water from 8 m to 64 m up. The well of water over the mound stays open to the surface.
