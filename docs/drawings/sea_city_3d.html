Sea Folk City in Depth

A walled and roofed city, 540 m north of the arrival dock. Its walls rise from the seabed, 72 m down, and stand 24 m out of the sea. On the roof a fountain plays, and inside its pedestal is the stair down. Under the roof are three eras. Spires of the first city still stand. The classical city is preserved on the floor. The modern glass city joins it, wraps it and climbs above it. Every form is a SuperEgg. Drag to look around, scroll to zoom, and pick a place to visit.
