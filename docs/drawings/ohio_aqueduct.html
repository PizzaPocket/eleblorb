Eleblorb · Ohio · water works, fifth version, proposed 12 Oct 2026

Ohio Water Plan

The terrain has a vertex only every 18 m (the grey dots), so it can level whole cells but cannot cut a channel. Everything here is built on the ground.
• The dam: the tarn's south bank is an earth dam exactly one cell wide.
• The mill: a timber launder carries water from the sluice to an overshot wheel at the dam's foot.
• The leat: the mill's spent water runs in a raised stone channel along the village's north-east edge and the east road. It goes out over the lake on a timber flume.
• Roads: no road crosses the water.
• Clean water: a buried main brings it to the Conduit house, then to every house and the fountain.
The plan is in town metres, with the fountain at the origin and north at the top.

The leat from the wheel to the rim

Heights are stretched about 18 times. The channel stands on the ground: its bed is always at or above the ground, falling 0.55% from 0.75 m to 0.05 m, with water 0.3 m deep and kerbs 0.5 m above the bed. So it is a low stone wall, 1.25 m high at the mill and 0.55 m at the rim, and it never needs the terrain cut.

Dam, launder and mill, true scale

The earth dam falls 9.6 m across exactly one terrain cell, from the crest vertex to the pad. A timber launder on trestles carries water 20 m from the sluice to the top of a 6 m overshot wheel, which stands in a masonry race on level ground beside the mill's north gable.

The flume, true scale

On the grid the cliff falls across one 18 m cell, so it is never vertical. The leat continues about 7 m past the lip on a timber flume with raking struts, the length the existing _flume_reach finds. Water leaving at 2.6 m/s falls about 62 m and lands about 16 m out, in the bay.
