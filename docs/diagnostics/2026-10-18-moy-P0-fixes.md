# Moy P0 fixes — 2026-10-18

## Result

The nine checks now share a physics-derived walk grid. The full Moy audit completed headlessly in 200.986 s (3 min 21 s), under the five-minute limit, over `-763,-203` to `-357,203`. It reported 5,086 findings. This is a new measurement baseline; the package improves how the suite measures geometry and does not repair those reported Moy geometry findings.

/Applications/Godot.app/Contents/MacOS/Godot --headless --rendering-method gl_compatibility --path . res://tools/world_checks.tscn -- --kingdom=moy --date=2026-10-18

The suite waits for the Moy packs, prunes the unrelated town and other kingdoms before tree entry, then runs on Moy and its terrain. Machine-readable and Markdown outputs are in `checks/moy-2026-10-18.json` and `checks/moy-2026-10-18.md`.

## Methods and baseline

| Check | Method | Findings | Time |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| `overlap` | Compare walk cells from distinct physics bodies at 0.25 m refinement; cluster cells within 2 cm and suppress pieces sharing a `walk_run_id`. | 936 | 13.872 s* |
| `buried` | Test each walk-grid sample against terrain height. | 0 | 25.270 s |
| `water` | Sample 0.5 m outside each water mesh's rectangular bounds and verify the expected shared water shader. This is an AABB perimeter approximation, not an exact circular shoreline test. | 0 | 0.022 s |
| `reach` | Flood fill the walk grid with floor-angle, 0.7 m step, 0.45 m footprint approximation, 2.0 m clearance, and obstruction checks; connect lift, escalator and train stops through builder tags and link ids. | 75 | 0.567 s |
| `trap` | Reverse-fill from the spawn region over the same graph; inspect walk cells in closed shells not tagged `enterable`. | 975 | 10.325 s |
| `headroom` | Cast clearance rays upward from reachable walk cells and cluster obstructions. | 28 | 4.547 s |
| `underside` | For deck/flight-like bodies, cast upward from 1 m below walk cells and test whether the hit face is visible from below. | 777 | 1.402 s |
| `solid` | Find visible solid meshes without nearby collision, honoring decorative `CollisionPolicy` metadata. | 0 | 0.104 s |
| `guard` | Sample walk-grid edges for missing guard outlines. | 2,295 | 5.845 s |

\* `overlap` includes the 11.331 s `overlap_grid` refinement time; it is not additive. Shared preflight took 26.091 s for the 0.5 m grid and 103.561 s for the reach graph. The grid covered 660,969 columns and 1,233,770 walk cells; the refined overlap grid contained 459,408 cells.

The 0.45 m player radius is represented conservatively by neighboring cardinal support cells rather than a swept capsule. Headroom is sampled from grid-supported cell centers. These discretizations can miss sub-grid details and may create borderline findings; inspect geometry in-game before treating an individual sample as definitive.
