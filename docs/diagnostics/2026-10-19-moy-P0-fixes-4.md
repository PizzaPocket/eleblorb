# Moy P0 fixes 4: the check suite, after section 12

2026-10-19. Package P0, section 12 of `docs/architecture/moy_pkg_P0.md`. Built by
the planning session at the user's request, in the same pass as P1's fourth
build, because the two share the walk grid. The suite and its fixtures changed;
the one builder change that touches a check is the helix deck's mesh name.

## What changed, by follow-up

1. **The baseline is reproducible from the repository.** The tread tag, P1's
   builder, validator, plan constants and the 205 rebaked packs are committed
   together with the suite. `checks/moy-2026-10-18.*` was restored (a probe run
   had overwritten it with a 21-line file); `moy-debug.*` and `moy-probe*.*` are
   removed. `build/web` is not touched.

2. **The 56 helix `underside` findings were a method fault, not missing
   geometry.** A helix hand is a closed section 0.9 m deep (`ESCALATORS.section`)
   with a soffit that also collides. The check failed it twice:
   - Its fallback thickness for a curved deck was capped at 0.75 m, so it looked
     for the soffit 0.15 m short of where it is. Every cell of every hand failed.
   - It then measured to the soffit by perpendicular distance. On a 27° flight
     the perpendicular foot of a top point near the high end falls past the end
     of the soffit, so the last cell at each end of a flight failed even with the
     right thickness (the 34 of 56 first failing cells that stood at a landing
     height).

   The fix: the thickness of a curved deck is the vertical distance to its own
   nearest down face under the sample, up to 1.5 m (`_own_down_face_depth`), and
   a soffit straight under the sample counts as visible. A deck with no down face
   under it still takes the old cap, so a missing soffit still fails. On the
   Crossing the 56 findings are now 0, and 0 on the civic core and the park deck.

   Each finding now carries its evidence: failing cells of total (and the share),
   which test failed, the span of heights, and the owning body in `other_path`.
   The helix deck's mesh is named `CrossingHandDeck`; the ids that changed every
   run are gone.

3. **The 12 `deck_visual_missing` findings are explained, and removed.**
   - Ten were the tops of glass balustrades, not decks: the collision shape is a
     box 9.20 × 0.95 × 0.11 m (the Clock Room arrival belts) or 16.82 × 0.95 ×
     0.11 m (City Hall's F1 flights). A face 0.11 m wide cannot be stood on. Cells
     whose supporting box face is narrower than the walker's radius (0.45 m) no
     longer need a visual.
   - Two were `Deck_Park Deck_0` and `_2`, 136 m long boxes. `_build_mesh_buckets`
     skipped any mesh over 128 m, so their visuals were never considered. The
     limit is 256 m.

   Each cell now records its collision shape (type, size, local height) and the
   width of the face it stands on.

4. **Smaller.** Each fixture line prints its own flags; the duplicate owner
   lookup was already consolidated by the earlier build.

## Three further changes the City Hall work forced

P1's reach findings could not be settled while the oracle could not see an
escalator. Reading cell by cell (`--cell-probe`), three faults showed up:

- **A balustrade hid its belt.** The walk grid takes the highest hit of a body in
  a column, then excludes the body. On a column through a belt's glass rail the
  highest hit is the rail's 0.11 m top, so the belt under it was never sampled.
  The grid now carries on down through a box top narrower than 0.45 m without
  excluding the body.
- **A 1.1 m belt kept at most one column.** The support filter asks for a walkable
  neighbour 0.5 m away on every side. Cells that fail it narrowly (with at least
  one neighbour present) are tested again with four rays at the walker's real
  radius, 0.45 m. The City Hall box gained about 3,000 walk cells.
- **A target was judged by its single nearest cell.** A low-headroom cell under a
  ramp could mask a reached cell half a metre away. A target now reads as
  reached when a reached cell lies within 0.8 m of it.

New options: `--reach-map=x0,z0,x1,z1,y0,y1` (an ASCII map of reached and
unreached cells in a box and height band), `--cell-probe=x,z;...` (every cell in
a column with its body, shape, clearance and the first thing overhead) and
`--debug-cell=x,z` (the raw hit and the support decision).

## Baseline

Command: `Godot --headless --rendering-method gl_compatibility --path .
res://tools/world_checks.tscn -- --kingdom=moy --date=2026-10-19`. Time **274 s**
(walk grid 38.5 s, reach graph 123.8 s, underside 39.6 s). It replaces
`checks/moy-2026-10-19.*` and is the measured "now" for the geometry of
2026-10-19 after P1's fourth build.

| Check | Previous baseline | Now |
|---|---:|---:|
| overlap | 429 | 403 (306 surface overlaps, 97 flush with terrain) |
| buried | 4 | 4 |
| water | 0 | 0 |
| reach | 112 | 52 |
| trap | 1 | 4 |
| headroom | 38 | 65 (45 blocking) |
| underside | 68 | **0** |
| solid | 219 | 219 |
| guard | 2,645 | 2,733 |

Cells: 1,407,287 walk cells; 902,925 reached from Civic Plaza; 12 trapped.

- `reach` fell by 60: City Hall's 34 are 0, and the narrow-belt cells recovered
  every SpineAccess landing at the gates and squares. The 52 left are the West
  Gate towers (42), four Crossing lift calls at the deck concourse, four Deck lift
  calls and two Deck ramp landings.
- `trap` rose because the oracle now walks the City Hall belts: the four findings
  are the outer pair's belt cells at its foot (3 cells each). The old single East
  Gate platform cell is gone.
- `headroom` rose because more cells are reached. The new blocking findings are
  the 2.0 to 2.2 m fringe under the low end of every flight (a flight over a walk
  floor always has one); they need a planning decision, listed in P1's
  diagnostic.
- `overlap`: the rain gardens' 12 flush-with-terrain findings are gone (97
  remain, as before).
