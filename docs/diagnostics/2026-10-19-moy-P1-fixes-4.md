## City Hall's front

The plan (city.md, "the front") has a Rostrum, a deck across the gate's opening,
two flights for the Steps with a shared half-landing, and a lift with a stop at
each level. The builder had none of the decks, one flight starting 7.1 m in the
air, and one call post a lift. Now:

- **The Rostrum:** a deck 7 m deep across the whole front at +14.2 with the
  speaker's bay (a polygon deck with a soffit and convex collision), and the
  deck across the opening to the Wall Line's inner platform. One run id, so the
  seam is not an overlap.
- **The Steps:** two flights, from the forecourt's paving (+0.13) to the
  half-landing at +7.1 and on to the Rostrum. The stair lane is 0.18 m risers,
  one box a step (the soffit a sawtooth, 0.9 m under each tread edge), in two
  flights a leg; the escalator lanes are pairs per flight.
- **Piers and lintel:** the piers were two solid 60 m walls (the code's comment
  said they left the bay and portals open). They now leave the bay, both Steps
  portals, the gate's opening and the legs' portals. The lintel was a 60 m beam
  that the scissor flights climbed through; it spans the opening only.
- **Levels:** the outer landing, the walk over the tracks and the half-landing
  stood 0.2 m above their plan level (the body was centred on the level, not
  hung from it); their tops are now at the plan level.
- **Landings:** each flight's pads were centred on the endpoint, half under the
  belt box, and ended 4 cm proud of the deck they sit on. For City Hall they are
  now 1.2 m deep, just past the flight's end and 4 cm under the deck (no
  coplanar tops). The reach target is a child on a lane's centre line, not the
  seam between two lanes.
- **Lifts:** a call post at every stop, on the door's side (`'w'` is west; a
  number is the z direction: `city_layout.py`, `Point(x_, z_+door*(h_+0.6))`).
  They were one post, ground level, east of the car whatever the door.
- **The leg's east wall:** the 9 m wall of the 12 to 21 m band stood where the
  plan puts the outer landing and the outer pair ("down the leg's east side, its
  landing inside the leg"), so both were inside a solid block. Only the west wall
  stands. The pair's head is one belt half-width (1.1 m) inside the landing's
  east edge: the plan draws it on the edge, so half the belts hung over it.
