# Moy package P2 diagnostic — 2026-10-19

**Verdict: Fix / remains in build.** The requested P2 source work, benchmark instrumentation, three comparable rendered runs, two unload/reload cycles per run, P0 audit, and image capture are complete. Acceptance is not met: dense-view frame times and geometry exceed targets, activation stalls repeat, nine hotel packs remain retained, and the required actual moving-train and player-controlled route suite has not been exercised. The original exit-134 launch was not reproduced on the hardware renderer; its cause remains unproven.

## Reproduction and renderer diagnosis

The first windowed launch in the restricted session exited 134 before producing output. It did not provide a crash log or native backtrace. Re-running through an approved GUI launch started normally on Godot 4.7.1-stable, `forward_plus`, Metal 4.0, Apple M5 (Apple9). Therefore the sandboxed exit is not evidence of a renderer/device crash. Headless checks use a dummy renderer and print RID/storage/mesh/base-type limitations; those are not treated as rendered evidence. The actual rendered runs reported visible geometry, completed frames and `accepted_rendering_evidence=true`.

Machine: macOS 26.5.1; MacBook Air, Apple M5 10-core CPU/GPU; 24 GiB RAM. Godot 4.7.1-stable official, Forward+, Metal 4.0. A separate driver version is not exposed by Godot. Commit `66127baf095558450293cb44254c86041d3cf764`.

The measured 9-pack retention came from hotel paths with pending requests that were not active when the player left the hotel radius. The hotel cleanup removed tracking only for active paths. It now also erases pending-request and request-time entries for every hotel path. A focused current-source rendered check ([r5 release check](raw/2026-10-19-moy-P2-rendered-r5-release-check.json), [log](raw/2026-10-19-moy-P2-rendered-r5-release-check.log)) completed two cycles with requested packs and retained manifest refs at zero after unload. Actor-group counts are not yet part of the probe, so duplicate residents/vendors are not ruled out and stable-node acceptance remains open. This release-check process overlapped briefly with the final headless runtime validator; do not use its frame timings as comparable performance data. The post-fix run still recorded >100 ms approach/reload events. Repeat the full three-process suite on final source.

## Required route and evidence gaps

The benchmark covers Crossroads, Old Moy street/rooftop, Downtown, Estate, Crossing/East Gate and a static train-hall view. The brief's real moving train ride through a station, turn and vertical crossing is not implemented in this probe. It also does not yet prove player-controlled fast flight at each gate, a cross-pack vine handover, occupied roof/platform/train support at unload limits, Xiao Hou Zi traversal, or entry while controlling another body. Those gates remain open; no browser atlas or static camera is substituted for them.

This includes overview, Old Moy street, Downtown, Estate, Crossing and existing flight/escalator route fixtures. The rig also ran its world-check preflight and emitted expected skips for fixtures without a reached top cell. The static train hall is not evidence of a moving ride.
