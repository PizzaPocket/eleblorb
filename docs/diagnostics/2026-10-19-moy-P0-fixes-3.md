# Moy P0 fixes 3 — 2026-10-19

Review target: P0.1 review in section 11 of `moy_pkg_P0.md`. This pass fixes
the check suite and shot rig; it does not change city geometry or update the
roadmap.

## Fixes

1. **Underside is evaluated per sampled cell.** For each walkable mesh, the
   check now requires every sampled cell to have a visible downward-facing
   face within that cell's deck thickness and an upward ray hit on that same
   body's collider. A missing cell fails the mesh; the finding records the
   failing sample position. The fixture covers a partially missing underside
   and a fully boxed clean twin. Both assertions pass.
2. **The old 161 findings were classified.** The 92 `HelixMovingTread` and 3
   `MovingTread` visuals are moving tread surfaces rather than structural
   decks. The builder tags these visuals `underside_visual_only`, and the
   underside check and shot target selection skip the tag. The fixture also
   includes a stepped flight with a tagged tread detail but no structural
   underside, plus a boxed clean twin; it catches the former and clears the
   latter. The 66 anonymous meshes were not tread visuals: the post-bake audit
   resolves the remaining failures to the Crossing's `CrossingHand` decks,
   listed below.
3. **Post-P1 baseline.** The full Moy audit was run after P1's geometry bake
   (rain-garden ring, park lawn openings and gallery rails, baked 07:41–07:42
   on 2026-10-19). The tread-tag metadata rebake at 07:58 did not change
   geometry. The audit JSON was written at 08:24 and replaces
   `checks/moy-2026-10-19.*`. This is the baseline against that geometry.
4. **Flight shots and dimensions.** Every skipped flight run logs its run id
   and reason. `under_high_line` has a dedicated street-level camera. The rig
   sets the viewport to 1280 × 720 and logs the saved dimensions.
5. **Smaller check-suite fixes.** The duplicate mesh-collision-owner lookup
   was consolidated into `_collision_owner_for_node`. Underside thickness
   selects the box collider whose top supports the sampled cell and preindexes
   body shapes instead of walking a body's subtree per candidate. A collider
   cell with no visual within 8 cm is now a `deck_visual_missing` finding.
