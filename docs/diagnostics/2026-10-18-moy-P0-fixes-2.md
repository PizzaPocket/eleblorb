# Moy P0 section 8 fixes — 2026-10-18

This diagnostic records the second review pass for the P0 checks and screenshot rig. The full city audit covers `x=-790..-330`, `z=-210..210`; the window was expanded to include the West Gate and City Hall endpoints. The 0.5 m walk grid sampled 774,561 columns and retained 1,403,461 walk cells. The 0.25 m overlap refinement retained 409,882 cells.

## Changes

1. **Reach start and fill totals.** The city builder adds `reach_start` at Civic Plaza's forecourt, at `(-380.00, -59.87, 0.00)`. The graph starts there and at station platforms. The report now includes the primary start, reachable cells and trapped-cell share.
2. **Escalator links.** Each pair of foot and head landings shares a `reach_link_id`. Any endpoint without a walk cell within 3 m, and any link with fewer than two distinct endpoints, becomes a `reach` finding.
3. **Underside winding and selection.** The underside-facing normal and closed-mesh winding checks use Godot's clockwise front-face convention. The check selects only bodies carrying `walk_deck` or `flight` metadata; it no longer guesses from names. Empty-surface meshes are safely skipped.
4. **Overlap runs.** The builder writes `walk_run_id` on emitted street, lane, deck, garden-bed, rim-wall, lot, transit-track, rail and helix segments. Overlap suppression uses only equal explicit run IDs. Terrain contacts remain in the baseline as kind `flush_with_terrain`, with detail “flush with terrain”.
5. **Missing collision.** `solid` grows a substantial visual's world bounds by 5 cm and checks those bounds against actual physics shapes. Shapes with a dimension over 64 m are excluded from the query.
6. **Fixture assertions.** Each fixture asserts its named fault and expected detail, and checks that its clean twin is not reported. `trap` separately asserts a one-way pocket and a reachable non-enterable shell with a gap. The reach fixture also asserts a broken link endpoint.
7. **P0 viewpoints.** `--package=P0` reads the ten IDs in section 9 from `moy_city_3d.html` `PLACES`, using the brief's mapping. It adds the overview, four eye-level street views and under-flight street views. The rig also groups tagged flight segments by `walk_run_id` and renders an under-flight view for each group. `under_high_line` is now above the street, looking up from below a flight.
8. **Baseline refresh.** The complete Moy run replaced `docs/diagnostics/checks/moy-2026-10-18.json` and `.md` with the corrected-area measurements below.

No Moy geometry was changed for these checks; the builder changes are metadata used by the audit and screenshot rig.
