# Moy P1 fixes, second review

2026-10-18. Package P1, section 5 of `docs/architecture/moy_prototype_pass_4.md`.

## Changes

- Replaced the eight half-beams with four single 14.8 m beams: two Spine beams along x and two high-line beams along z. Their ends are at ±7.4 m from the relevant centreline.
- Added `--p1-only` to `tools/validate_moy_runtime.gd`. It loads the baked Crossing and civic-core cells only, then runs the beam, helix-band, way-out and gallery checks. This avoids the full streaming path while checking the actual collision packs.
- The band check walks every hand profile in increments no greater than 5°, querying the 2.6 m headroom band with 0.4 m slices. Only `CrossingHand_*` is exempt.
- The way-out check now queries r=4–7 m, 2.4 m wide, from floor +0.05 m to +2.8 m.
- Replaced the hard-coded gallery collider lookup with a type-based lookup and reports actual gallery counts.
