## Deviations and remaining verification

- The mech uses the game's `ProceduralFigure` joint family at 1.05 scale,
  exposes its rig-neutral pivot map, owns a `TraversalContext` and
  `TraversalDirector`, and participates in the three-driver parity check.
  The parity check is static; this pass did not manually exercise every
  power on the mech. Its shell/visor and armor are first-pass procedural
  geometry rather than a verified match to the model's exact curved visor
  cut, SuperEgg tube radii, and hinge motion.
- The plan's louvre, lift and zone data agree with the brief. There is no
  P0 reach/collision audit for the climb or arena yet. The 30%-ring balcony
  leap, 55%-ring rail, glass collision and crown-rib landings still need a
  rendered walk test.
- P17's win is held in the autoloaded `WorldState` and survives streamed
  scene re-entry in the same run. The repository has no general save-game
  serialization API for these campaign flags, so a process restart / load
  round-trip was not established.

This is a source-and-smoke-test pass, not L3 acceptance. The visual
play-through and the remaining items above are needed before marking P17
done.
