# Southeast Asian limestone karst flora (Phang Nga towers)

For the fishing village's islets (Anvil Rock and Heron Rock) and any other tower
karst. The model is the limestone towers of Phang Nga Bay and Krabi: sheer rock
rising from water, thin soil only in cracks, ledges and on the crowns, so the
flora is drought-hardy and rock-rooted even in a wet climate. The Crossroads lake
is fresh water, so the sea's mangroves and salt plants at the foot are left out.

## Zones, from the water up

| Zone | Conditions | Plants | Reads as |
|---|---|---|---|
| Notch (waterline to 2 m) | wet, shaded, undercut by the water | bare stained rock; moss; a few bird's-nest ferns tucked in the overhang | a dark band with green flecks |
| Faces (sheer) | almost no soil; seepage streaks | fig roots plastered down the rock in braided strands; hanging lianas and root curtains from the ledges and crowns above; mosses along seepage streaks | streaked rock laced with roots and vines |
| Ledges | pockets of soil on steps in the face | dragon trees (*Dracaena*) in clumps; cycads (*Cycas*); bird's-nest ferns; a few small figs | sculptural tufts standing out against the rock |
| Crowns | thin soil, exposed, dry spells | low, dense, gnarled karst forest (figs and small evergreen trees) with dragon trees emerging above it | a tight green cap, not a lawn |

## Plants

| Plant | Term | Status | Form for the builder |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dragon tree | *Dracaena* (karst species) | native | a grey trunk forking once or twice into candelabra branches, each tipped with a tuft of stiff strap leaves |
| Cycad | *Cycas* | native | a short stout trunk under a crown of arching feather fronds |
| Bird's-nest fern | *Asplenium nidus* | native | a vase-shaped rosette of broad bright leaves |
| Strangler fig | *Ficus* | native | on faces, braided roots running down the rock; on crowns, a broad dense canopy |
| Lianas | woody climbers | native | thin strands hanging in curtains from ledge and crown lips |
| Karst scrub-forest | small evergreen trees | native | low rounded crowns in tight clusters |

## People's plants

The village is built against the rock, not on it, so its plants live on its
decks: Pree's herb floats (lemongrass, mint, basil) at the Aran veranda, and
Asha's clinic herbs in pots on the Sen porch. The seep behind the cistern keeps
a patch of moss and ferns wet at the rock foot.

## Rules

- No lawn and no flower beds on the rock: soil exists only on ledges and crowns.
- Plant in clumps (three to seven dragon trees or cycads per ledge pocket), never
  evenly along a ledge.
- The two islets differ: Anvil Rock, larger and shaded on its village side,
  carries more fern and liana; Heron Rock, small and exposed, carries more dragon
  trees and cycads and a thinner crown.
