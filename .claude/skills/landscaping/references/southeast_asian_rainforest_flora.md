# Southeast Asian rainforest flora for the Plant Kingdom

A working palette for the Plant (Primate) Kingdom's jungle, modelled on the
lowland rainforests of Borneo, Sumatra and the Malay Peninsula, used by
`docs/architecture/plant_kingdom_landscape.md`. Names give the Malay or
Indonesian name, the common name and the botanical name. The village's
residents take their names from the same languages.

## Status, and why it matters

- **Native:** grows wild in these forests.
- **Old introduction:** brought from elsewhere in Asia long ago and now part of
  every village garden (mango, jasmine, pomegranate). Use around homes and
  gardens, not in the wild forest.
- **Legendary:** the old peach trees of Flower Fruit Mountain. Peaches are a
  temperate fruit and would not grow in this forest. They grow there only
  because of Sun Wu Kong (see the landscape plan), and nowhere else in the
  kingdom.
- **Excluded:** invasive species that damage real Southeast Asian forests.
  Never use them, even as filler:
  - *Mikania micrantha* (mile-a-minute);
  - *Chromolaena odorata* (Siam weed);
  - *Lantana camara*;
  - *Clidemia hirta* (Koster's curse);
  - *Acacia mangium*;
  - *Leucaena leucocephala*;
  - water hyacinth;
  - the African tulip tree;
  - *Mimosa pigra*.
  
  Avoid blanket *lalang* grass (*Imperata cylindrica*) too. It is native, but
  it marks burnt, degraded ground, not a living forest.
- **Out of region:** the **baobab** (*Adansonia*) is African and Australian.
  The current jungle scatter uses it; the landscape plan proposes replacing it.

## Climate and zones

Hot, wet and still all year, with rain most afternoons and no dry season to
speak of. The forest is layered: emergents tower over a closed canopy, with a
dim understorey of palms and saplings and a ground layer of gingers, aroids and
ferns. Zones follow water, light and rock:

| Zone | Conditions | Typical plants |
|---|---|---|
| Riverbank | flood-scoured banks, light from the open water | bamboo clumps, figs leaning over the water, *pandan*, rheophyte shrubs, gingers, ferns on boulders |
| Lowland dipterocarp forest | deep shade, rich soil | emergents (*tualang*, *meranti*, *keruing*), canopy (durian, figs, *rambutan*, *cempedak*), *rotan* and fan palms beneath, gingers, aroids and ferns on the ground |
| Gap and clearing | full sun where a giant fell | *mahang* pioneers, wild banana, torch ginger, tall grass and ferns |
| Limestone karst | thin soil on rock, cliffs | figs rooting on rock, *periuk kera* (pitcher plants), begonias, cycads, mosses and ferns in cracks |
| Spray zone | constant mist from falls | moss, liverworts, filmy ferns, begonias, balsams |
| Village garden | cleared, tended | bananas, fruit trees, gingers, lemongrass, *pandan*, taro, jasmine |
